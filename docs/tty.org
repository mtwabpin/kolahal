#+TITLE: TTY mode
#+STARTUP: showall
#+TAGS: seam engine screens log home release extras shaders scripts

The TTY home style: an opt-in home where the log page and its parts are drawn on a strict
character grid, like a real terminal, and the later shader option on top of it. The design is in
[[file:tty-architecture.md][tty-architecture.md]]; its first section has the maintainer's decisions of 21 November 2026, which
override the rest. Internal: nothing here is published.

- One step at a time, in order; each leaves master shippable. Steps 0 and 1 can run in parallel.
- Sizes: S up to 2 days, M about a week, L about two weeks.
- Write what to do after =Remark:= (do it, skip it, or change it), and I'll follow that.

* Decided
- TTY is opt-in and drops what a grid can't do: no images (alt text, initials or names instead), one
  font size (bold or inverse for emphasis; =font.size= and pinch still size the whole grid).
- The widgets page always stays GUI.
- The GUI gets nothing because TTY has it (no scroll thumb, no selection mode in the GUI).
- =home.style= stays a config key (=config set home.style tty=) until step 4, then goes into Settings.
