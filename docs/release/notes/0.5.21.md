Kolahal 0.5.21, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.20 and keeps your data and settings.

This release has no APKs: while Kolahal is in testing on Google Play, you install and update it from there. That is the Play build: no Assistant, no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts.

## What's new

- **The top of a widgets page takes less room.** The row with the page's name, + and the pencil is gone. The page's name sits in one top corner and a mark for each of home's pages in the other, in one thin line that stays in place while you swipe. With the status bar hidden on a phone with a camera hole, they sit beside the camera, in the strip that was left empty: the first widget starts 48 dp higher there, and 28 dp higher otherwise.
- **An empty widgets page stays, and says what to do.** A page you add in Arrange widgets was removed on the way back when it had no widget. It now stays until you remove it. Home shows it with a picture in the style of the app's icon, **No widgets yet**, an **Add a widget** button that adds to that page, and **Remove page**.
- **Shortcuts show their own icons.** Hold an app and its shortcuts in the menu each carry the icon the app gave them, as on any launcher.
- **Two focused guides for an AI assistant.** `skill.md shaders` copies a guide to shaders alone, and `skill.md widgets` one for widget scripts, with the whole script API and a complete script for each kind of card. `skill.md` alone copies the whole guide, as before. The guide's snippet for a scene behind the text is corrected: written from the old one, a scene could paint over pictures and icons.

## Changed

- **The widgets page has no + and no pencil.** Arrange widgets opens by holding a widget, as before, or with `widgets`, the log header's menu or Settings; **Add widget** is in its top bar, and `widget add` opens the picker. An empty page has its own **Add a widget**. With TalkBack, both are actions on the top line of the page.
- **`@on notification, com.example.chat` runs for that app.** A comma after `notification` made the script run for every app. A script that relied on that should say `@on notification` alone.
- **A backup's empty widgets pages come back.** Importing a backup used to leave out a page that got no widget.
- **The widget templates and World clocks say `widget add`** where they said to tap +. Their versions are raised (templates 1.1, World clocks 1.2), so an unedited copy is offered the update.

## Fixed

- **A widget can go at the bottom of its page.** In Arrange widgets a drop stopped just under the other widgets, so a widget alone on a page stayed at the top. It can now be dropped anywhere down to the foot of the page, and the room above it stays until you use Close gaps.
- **The widgets page's marks show home's pages in their order.** They showed only the widgets pages, in a fixed order. There is a mark for every page now, in the order set under Page order, with the log and the Assistant's page as small icons.
- **Home could close after you came back to it.** With the widgets pages off, opening a screen from the Assistant's page and pressing Back crashed home, and so did turning the widgets pages off while on one. Home now returns to the page you were on, the Assistant's too, and to the log when that page is gone.
- **Deleting an old notification's line no longer dismisses a newer notification.** A chat's later messages share one notification with the first: deleting the first message's line dismissed it with the unread ones. A notification is dismissed only when the line is about what is showing now.
- **A line swipe that does nothing on a line leaves the swipe to the page.** With a swipe set to run or fresh, lines it couldn't act on took the swipe and the page never turned.
- **Right-to-left: a log line follows the finger.**
- **Fresh from here doesn't count a locked private space's lines.**
- **Undo in Arrange widgets can't put a widget on a removed page.** The widgets stayed hidden until the app restarted.
- **Album art as a background is sharper on wide cards.**
- **`@on notification` with commas means the apps named.** `@on notification com.example.chat, org.example.mail` runs for both; `@on notification, com.example.chat` ran for every app and now runs for that one.
- **A one-off shader carries on when you leave the Shaders screen** instead of starting again on home.
- **`web.open` reached another way in JavaScript**, such as `const { open } = web`, no longer ends at an allow button that can't work.
- **The Assistant (full build):** a key with an invisible character is refused where you enter it, and no error can show a key; a key never follows a redirect; opening or deleting a conversation while a question waits no longer mixes or brings back conversations; the conversation stays when you leave the page, and a cancelled question is really cancelled; a broken rich answer shows as a failed answer.
