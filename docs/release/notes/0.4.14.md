Kolahal 0.4.14, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.13 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.14.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.14-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **The pinned header**: with the header off, a line at the top of home shows what's playing and what scripts pin there with `pin(text)`, such as a live score. With more than one, they take turns in that line, each for 5 seconds (**Pinned header: seconds per item**, `header.pinned-seconds`), with dots at the end for up to 3 (**Pinned header: dots up to**, `header.pinned-dots`). A finger on the line keeps the one showing. A script's item can have a bar for how far along it is, and can ask to run again as often as every 10 seconds while home shows it. Tap an item to run its script, long-press it for its menu.
- **Live cricket and football**: two new scripts under Get more scripts, `cricket` and `football`, follow a team's live match and pin its score, with the overs or the minutes as the bar. `cricket live` and `football live` list the live matches. Both read ESPN's scores.

## Removed

- **Now playing with the header off** (`header.media-always`): what's playing always shows in the pinned header now when the header is off.

**kolahal-0.4.14-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
