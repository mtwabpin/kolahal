Kolahal 0.4.14, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.13 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.14.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.14-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **A one-line header**: the header is one line now, a status bar of its own: the time and date on the left, and the battery and two items you choose on the right (**First item** and **Second item**, `header.item-1` and `header.item-2`: the next alarm and open todos by default, or the network, today's unlocks, a settings button or the work and private profiles). In full screen it sits beside the camera, in the band the camera leaves across the top, so the log gets that room; with the status bar shown, it sits under it. It looks the same all the time: no shrinking while you type, no hiding as you scroll back. What doesn't fit beside the camera is left out, your second item first. The next alarm shows just its time when it's within a day.
- **The pinned header**: under the header, one line for what's playing and for the scripts you pin, such as a live score. With more than one, they take turns in that line, each for 5 seconds (**Seconds per item**, `header.pinned-seconds`), with dots at the end for up to 3 (**Dots up to**, `header.pinned-dots`). A finger on the line keeps the one showing, and it steps aside while you type. With the header off, it's the only line at the top.
- **You choose which scripts show at the top**: a script's status line shows on its card in Scripts and in `status`, and at the top only once you pin it: **Pin to the top** in the long-press menu of any line it logged, the **Pinned at the top** switch on its card, or `script pin <name>` (`script unpin <name>` takes it away). A script can't pin itself. Tap a pinned line to run its script; long-press it for Run, Unpin and Edit.
- **`status(text, opts)`**: a script's status line can have a bar for how far along it is (`progress`, 0 to 1), and can ask to run again as often as every 10 seconds while home shows it (`refresh`); such a run gets `event.name` `"refresh"`. Older versions ignore the options.
- **Live cricket and football**: two scripts under Get more scripts, `cricket` and `football`, follow a team's live match and keep its score as their status line, with the overs or the minutes as the bar. Pin one to see the score at the top. `cricket live` and `football live` list the live matches. Both read ESPN's scores.
- **Swipe a feed item** in Feeds to mark it read without opening it, or a read one unread again.
- **A feed's address can be changed**: **Edit address** in its sheet in Feeds, or `feed url <id> <url>`, for one added with a typo or one that moved. Its items and settings stay, and it's fetched again at once.

## Removed

- **The full header and the one-line header**, with their settings: the clock, date and battery switches (always shown now), each item's switch for each header (now the two items), auto-hide, labels instead of icons, script status lines in the header (now the ones you pin), name@device in the header (`fetch` still shows it), and **Now playing with the header off** (`header.media-always`: what's playing always shows in the pinned header). Your items carry over: the first two that were on, in the order the header showed them. Scripts whose line the header showed are pinned once, so none goes without a word; unpin what you don't want.

**kolahal-0.4.14-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
