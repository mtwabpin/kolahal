Kolahal 0.5.0, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.16 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.0.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.0-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## Changed

- **A new icon**: stacked answers, in the old icon's colours. A big number and a range bar, each beside its coloured line as in the log, over the prompt and its cursor. `fetch` draws it too.
- **Big numbers**: `fmt.stats(items)` shows a few numbers large, each with its label under it, the first the biggest. `weather` uses it (the temperature with the sky under it, then humidity, wind and the UV index). So do `countdown` (the days to go, with the date and weekday), `sun` (the daylight left, sunrise and sunset), `aqi` (the index in its band's colour, the 24-hour figure, PM2.5), `crypto` and `exchange` for one coin or currency, `charge-reminder` (the battery, what the charge added, how long), `cricket` and `football` (the score), and, among the shared scripts, `moon` and `holidays`. On an older Kolahal, the shared scripts show what they did before.
- **Big numbers in commands too**: `date` shows the time large with the date under it, and `date tokyo` the time there. The calculator shows its result large (`= 5 km in mi`, `= 100 usd in eur`, `= 2^10`), with what you typed under it; a result too long for big type stays a line of text. `unlocks` shows today's count large (and, for several days, the average and the total above the bars), and `usage` the screen time. Copy still gives the plain result.
- **Space between the items of a list**: a small gap (`log.item-spacing`, 5 dp by default) between items that take more than a line, with the colour bar running through it, so they stand apart but stay one group: `calls`, `notes`, `reminders`, `installs`, `crashes`, `feed read`, `notifs`, `feeds`, `script ls`, `script lib`, `setup text`, the stories of `hn`, `lobsters`, `reddit` and `releases`, and, among the shared scripts, `define` and `rss-digest`. `help`, `agenda` and `config` get it above each section only. Scripts ask for it with `ctx.gap`.
- **More tags and bars in commands**: `script access`, `dnd status` and `rotate status` show on and off as tags, as `toggles` does; `script secret` tags set and not set; `script lib` and `script get` tag what you have (installed, edited, update); `script ls` and `feeds` tag what is switched off. `toggles` draws bars for volume and brightness, and `media` for how far the track has played.
- **Buttons under a script's line**: `ctx.actions` puts up to 3 buttons, as soft pills, under a line. A tap runs that script again with the button's arguments (`event.name` is `"action"`), never another command, and the line then shows that button as done.
- **People have pictures**: a call in the log (and in `calls`) and a contact in `contacts` start with the person's photo, round. Without a photo, or when Kolahal may not read contacts, their first letters show on a colour of the theme, the same for a person every time; `#` stands for a number with no name.
- **Lobsters in the script library**: `lobsters` logs the new stories on lobste.rs's front page, like `hn` does Hacker News's, and notifies you about the top one. Each story shows up to two of its tags, its score and a link to its comments, so you can open the story or the discussion; a feed of Lobsters in `rss-digest` could open only one of them.
- **`hn` shows each story's title first**: its points come after the title, muted, with the link to its comments, where they were first and in bold.
- **`log.line-height` is only in config now**: it left Settings → Log; `config set log.line-height <n>` still sets it.
- **`charge-reminder`'s bar runs from where the charge started**: when you unplug, its bar goes from the battery level at which you plugged in to the level now, on the 0–100% scale, with both levels beside it. It went from 0 before.

## Fixed

- **A run you start that shows nothing now says so**: `script run hn` when there were no new stories ended at "Running hn…", as if it hung. Now it ends with "hn ran: nothing to show". The same goes for any script you run that logs, posts and returns nothing.
- **A search while a script updates a line**: with `/` open, each change a script made to a line in place (`line.set`) searched the whole log again, about every 300 ms. Now only the changed lines are read again: one that still matches shows its new text, and one that no longer matches leaves.

**kolahal-0.5.0-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
