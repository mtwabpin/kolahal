Kolahal 0.5.0, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.16 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.0.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.0-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## Changed

- **Lobsters in the script library**: `lobsters` logs the new stories on lobste.rs's front page, like `hn` does Hacker News's, and notifies you about the top one. Each story shows up to two of its tags, its score and a link to its comments, so you can open the story or the discussion; a feed of Lobsters in `rss-digest` could open only one of them.
- **`hn` shows each story's title first**: its points come after the title, muted, with the link to its comments, where they were first and in bold.
- **`charge-reminder`'s bar runs from where the charge started**: when you unplug, its bar goes from the battery level at which you plugged in to the level now, on the 0–100% scale, with both levels beside it. It went from 0 before.
- **People have pictures**: a call in the log (and in `calls`) and a contact in `contacts` start with the person's photo, round. Without a photo, or when Kolahal may not read contacts, their first letters show on a colour of the theme, the same for a person every time; `#` stands for a number with no name.
- **Buttons under a script's line**: `ctx.actions` puts up to 3 buttons, as soft pills, under a line. A tap runs that script again with the button's arguments (`event.name` is `"action"`), never another command, and the line then shows that button as done.
- **Big numbers**: `fmt.stats(items)` shows a few numbers large, each with its label under it, the first the biggest. `weather` uses it (the temperature with the sky under it, then humidity, wind and the UV index), and so does `countdown`: the days large, with the date and its weekday beside them, and the next one at the top of its list.

## Fixed

- **A search while a script updates a line**: with `/` open, each change a script made to a line in place (`line.set`) searched the whole log again, about every 300 ms. Now only the changed lines are read again: one that still matches shows its new text, and one that no longer matches leaves.

**kolahal-0.5.0-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
