Kolahal 0.4.16, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.15 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.16.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.16-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## Changed

- **The theme builder**: dragging a colour slider redraws only the preview, not the whole page under it. The Scripts page also redraws less when a script runs while it's open.

## Fixed

- **Script secrets**: a script could put a key's placeholder in a site's name (`https://<key>.example.com`). The key then went to the address lookup unencrypted, and the lookup's error showed it to the script. A key can't be part of a site's name now, and an error from a request never shows a key's value to the script, in Lua or JavaScript.
- **Importing a backup** no longer brings in a copy of recent notifications for scripts, or a script's stored values under its number, even when the file has them: this phone's copy stays, and stored values come back by the script's name, as before.
- **A command's output after a slow reply**: a currency conversion (`= 10 usd in eur`), `backup`, `script get`, `script export` and `import`, and `open … --resume` printed lines that weren't marked as the command's output, so after a few seconds they showed as lines of their own, and **Copy output** and **Delete command and output** missed them.
- **`feed every`** suggested `off`, which it doesn't take; `feed off <id>` switches a feed off.
- **`stopwatch` and `alarms`** opened the clock app without Enter once you'd typed enough of them, with `launch.instant` on `all`. They wait for Enter now, as `timer` and `alarm` do.
- **`script every x OFF`** in capitals was refused, while `30M` worked.
- **A script running twice at once**: run from the prompt while its timer was running, or with a daily time meeting its interval, its two runs shared its stored values, so one could undo the other's, and `rss.fresh` could show items again as new. A script's runs go one after the other now.
- **`feed fetch`** while another fetch was still waiting for a network or running said "Fetching…" and did nothing. It fetches after the one before now, and so does a feed whose address you changed meanwhile.
- **Repeating reminders after a time zone change**: when the zone changed while Kolahal wasn't running (after a force stop, or when Android had closed it), a `remind every day 9:00` went on at the old zone's 9:00. It moves to 9:00 in the new zone at the next start.
- **Crash reports' times** followed the phone's 12/24-hour setting; they follow `clock.24h` now, as the log's do.
- **Styled text from other apps**: a notification, an app's name or a feed could carry the invisible marks scripts use for bold, colours and lists, and show styled in the log. They're taken out now; only scripts style their lines.

**kolahal-0.4.16-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
