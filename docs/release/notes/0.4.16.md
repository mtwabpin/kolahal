Kolahal 0.4.16, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.15 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.16.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.16-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## Changed

- **"Scroll to bottom"**: when you scroll up the log, a soft pill above the prompt takes you back to the newest lines, in place of the round arrow button.
- **A status line's bar can take a colour**: `status(text, { progress, color })` takes one of the theme's roles (`good`, `warn`, `bad` and the others `fmt.color` takes), so a pinned bar can be orange or red when something is wrong. Without one, it stays the theme's accent.
- **Air quality in the script library**: `aqi` comes with Kolahal. Its pinned status line has a coloured bar: it fills while the air is good, and past that it is full, orange or red by how bad the air is. Its readings show as a table.
- **`hn` keeps no status line**, so it no longer offers to be pinned: a list of stories has nothing to show at the top of home. A script whose new code sets no status line drops its old one.
- **Live cricket and football in the script library**: `cricket` and `football` (until now only under **Get more scripts**) come with Kolahal, and `cricket live` and `football live` show their matches as a table. A cricket side that is all out fills its bar, and in a chase the bar follows the side chasing. A team's exact name now comes first: `cricket India` (or the team option `India`) finds India's match, not India A's or India Women's, and `Mumbai` still finds Mumbai Indians. A copy you installed from Get more scripts stays as it is: `script install cricket --replace` (or `football`) switches it to the library's, keeping its timer, options and pin.
- **The templates in the script library**: the five `template-…` scripts (a command, a timer, an event, an API key and JavaScript) come with Kolahal, as starting points for your own.
- **The pinned header is shorter**: 30 dp where it was 54, with the bar just under the text, so the log has more room. The whole line, bar included, takes a tap.
- **The pinned header shows a script's text alone**: `NZ 81/5 (26.5 ov) v IND`, not `cricket ▸ NZ 81/5 …`, so the line has room for it. A screen reader still says the script's name, and long-pressing the line names it.
- **Tables in the log**: a script can log a table as a line's body with `fmt.table(rows, { header, align })`. It shows in the terminal font with its columns lined up, boxed on a shaded background with lines between rows, or as plain columns (**Settings → Log → Tables**, `log.tables`). A table wider than the screen scrolls sideways, and **Copy text** copies it with tabs between the cells. `forecast` and the `countdown` list now show as tables, and `crypto` and `exchange` do when they show more than one row.
- **Fewer bold lines from scripts**: a script's line is bold only when it has a body under it, as a heading over the details. A line on its own, like each Hacker News story or each price, is now in normal weight, so a list is easier to read. The library scripts also use less bold: `forecast` no longer bolds every day, `reddit` and `releases` show one plain line per post or release, and headings no longer bold a word inside them.
- **When Kolahal isn't your home app**, a band above the prompt says so. Tap it and Android asks whether to make Kolahal the home app (on Android 9, or if you say no, its home app settings open instead). The band goes once Kolahal is the home app.
- **Arrange the header**: in **Settings → Header → Arrange the header**, every part (the time, date, battery, next alarm, todos, network, unlocks, profiles and the settings button) goes on the left or the right side, in the order you drag it to, or isn't shown. In the terminal, `config header.left` and `config header.right` take the parts separated by commas. Your two items (`header.item-1`, `header.item-2`) carry over to the right side, before the battery; those settings are gone. What doesn't fit now goes from the middle out, so the outermost parts stay longest.
- **Settings → Header shows the top of home**: the header and the pinned header, live, above the settings that change them, so you see an item, the 24-hour clock or the header itself come and go as you pick. It shows while the header is on.
- **The header sits below the status bar's area**, in full screen too (**Below the status bar area**, `header.below-bar`, on by default). Turn it off to have the header beside the camera in full screen, as before.
- **New defaults: the status bar shows, and the header is off** (`screen.bars show`, `header.show off`). If you never changed them, home now starts under the status bar with no header line; turn them back in **Settings → Look & feel → Full screen** and **Settings → Header → Show the header**, or with `config screen.bars immersive` and `config header.show on`.
- **Now playing is always on** in the pinned header, with notification access: its setting (`header.media`) is gone. If you had turned it off, what's playing shows again.
- **The app selector slides through apps** by default (`selector.browse apps`): the whole list, an app at a time when you slide slowly. If you never changed it, set **Settings → App selector → Slide through** to `letters` for a letter at a time.
- **The app selector's rows are taller** by default: 50 dp (`selector.row-height`), where they were 38, so the names are easier to hit. If you never changed it, set **Settings → App selector → Row height** back to 38.
- **The app selector's strip is longer** by default: 400 dp of the edge (`selector.height`), where it was 350. If you never changed it, set **Settings → App selector → Length on the edge** back to 350.
- **The letters show on the edge** by default (`selector.letters`), so you can see where to touch. If you never changed it, turn off **Settings → App selector → Show letters on the edge** for the handle alone.
- **App icons show in the selector's list** by default (`selector.icons`). If you never changed it, turn off **Settings → App selector → Show app icons** for names alone.
- **The app selector uses the phone's font** by default (`selector.system-font`). If you never changed it, turn off **Settings → App selector → System font** for the terminal's font.
- **Settings shows words, not raw values**: a choice's buttons read "Off", "Prompt only", "DuckDuckGo", and the themes "Green", "Gruvbox Material", where they showed `show`, `prompt`, `duckduckgo` and `gruvbox-material`. **Full screen**'s first choice now reads Off (it was `show`). `config` takes the same values as before.
- **The pinned header's timing** (`header.pinned-seconds`, `header.pinned-dots`) has left Settings → Header; set it with `config`, as before.
- **The theme builder**: dragging a colour slider redraws only the preview, not the whole page under it. The Scripts page also redraws less when a script runs while it's open.
- **Big unit conversions** are written in full, with commas, up to 15 digits: `= 1 gb in b` gives `1 GB = 1,000,000,000 B`, where it gave `1.000e+09 B`.

## Fixed

- **A crash while scrolling the log**: scrolling could stop Kolahal ("LayoutNode not found in RectList") once the log had lines with an app or notification icon in them. Each line's time was lined up with the line by asking Compose for the line's baseline, and with an icon in the line that crashed Compose as the line scrolled into view. The time is now lined up by measuring the two fonts instead, in the same place as before. 0.4.15 had this too.
- **`script unpin` suggested every script**: it now suggests only the scripts that are pinned. `script pin` suggests only the scripts that keep a status line and aren't pinned yet.
- **A script listed twice in a backup or a script folder**: the second copy found the first one's code already in place, so it counted as unchanged, and the file could switch the new script on. New and changed scripts now stay off until you review them, however often a file lists them. In a folder, two files with one name (`Foo.lua` and `foo.lua`) count the same way.
- **A backup with a value too big to read back**: an edited file could hold a row larger than Kolahal ever writes (over 2 MB). After the restore, home couldn't read its log or status lines again, and backups failed. Such rows are now left out of a restore, and the restore cleans up oversized values as an update does.
- **Returning to home is lighter**:
  - With suggestions hidden, home no longer ranks every app each time you come back.
  - The app selector sorts its list faster when home comes back from a settings screen.
  - The check for whether Kolahal is the home app runs off the main thread.
  - A script run that logs nothing no longer trims the log.
- **Script secrets**: a script could put a key's placeholder in a site's name (`https://<key>.example.com`). The key then went to the address lookup unencrypted, and the lookup's error showed it to the script. A key can't be part of a site's name now, and an error from a request never shows a key's value to the script, in Lua or JavaScript.
- **Importing a backup** no longer brings in a copy of recent notifications for scripts, or a script's stored values under its number, even when the file has them: this phone's copy stays, and stored values come back by the script's name, as before.
- **A command's output after a slow reply**: a currency conversion (`= 10 usd in eur`), `backup`, `script get`, `script export` and `import`, and `open … --resume` printed lines that weren't marked as the command's output, so after a few seconds they showed as lines of their own, and **Copy output** and **Delete command and output** missed them.
- **`feed every`** suggested `off`, which it doesn't take; `feed off <id>` switches a feed off.
- **`stopwatch` and `alarms`** opened the clock app without Enter once you'd typed enough of them, with `launch.instant` on `all`. They wait for Enter now, as `timer` and `alarm` do.
- **`script every x OFF`** in capitals was refused, while `30M` worked.
- **Importing a script file over one you have**: a changed `@every` or `@at` line didn't change its timer, as it does when you edit the script or import a script folder.
- **A script running twice at once**: run from the prompt while its timer was running, or with a daily time meeting its interval, its two runs shared its stored values, so one could undo the other's, and `rss.fresh` could show items again as new. A script's runs go one after the other now.
- **`feed fetch`** while another fetch was still waiting for a network or running said "Fetching…" and did nothing. It fetches after the one before now, and so does a feed whose address you changed meanwhile.
- **Repeating reminders after a time zone change**: when the zone changed while Kolahal wasn't running (after a force stop, or when Android had closed it), a `remind every day 9:00` went on at the old zone's 9:00. It moves to 9:00 in the new zone at the next start.
- **Crash reports' times**, and the last runs `script ls` shows, followed the phone's 12/24-hour setting; they follow `clock.24h` now, as the log's do.
- **Usage lines**: a command given a word it doesn't take says the usage line `help` shows. `script`'s also lists `lib`, `install`, `set`, `dir`, `export` and `import`, which it had left out.
- **`… | grep`** could refuse a pattern with "earlier patterns are still running" just after a few quick greps, though none of them was slow.
- **Styled text from other apps**: a notification, an app's name or a feed could carry the invisible marks scripts use for bold, colours and lists, and show styled in the log. They're taken out now; only scripts style their lines.

**kolahal-0.4.16-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
