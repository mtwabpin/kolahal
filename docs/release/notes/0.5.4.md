Kolahal 0.5.4, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.3 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.4.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.4-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## New

- **Birthdays and anniversaries in `countdown`**: `countdown add yearly 2001-06-15 Wedding anniversary` counts to the next 15 June every year, and says which one it is ("Wedding anniversary · 25th"). A date without a year (`countdown add 06-15 Mum's birthday`) comes every year too. A date you already saved stays one-time: remove it (`countdown rm`) and add it again with `yearly`.

## Changed

- **The app selector is always on the widgets page** when it is on (`selector.rail`). Before, it was there only when home opened on the widgets page (`widgets.start`).
- **The app selector uses the whole screen on the widgets page**: its list runs from under the status bar down to the navigation bar. Before, it stopped where the log's header and prompt would be. The strip stays where it is on the log, so your thumb finds it in the same place on both pages.

## Fixed

- **Space between events in the log**: each notification, and each app you open with a tap, now has the space above it (`log.spacing`) that a command's output has. Before, lines that came within 3 seconds of each other joined one group: two apps opened quickly, or a burst of notifications from one app (as when the work profile turns on), showed as one block with no space between them.
- **Suggestions for `hide`, `unhide`, `pin` and `unpin`** offer only the apps each one can change. `unhide` lists your hidden apps and `unpin` your pinned ones as soon as you type the space; `pin` offers your most used apps not pinned yet. Before, `unhide` and `unpin` showed nothing until you typed a letter, `pin` offered apps already pinned, and earlier lines could offer an app already hidden or pinned.
- **These commands act on an app they can change**: `unpin ma` unpins the pinned app that matches, even when another app matches better. When none can be changed, they say so ("Maps is pinned already") instead of doing nothing.

**kolahal-0.5.4-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
