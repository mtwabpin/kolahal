Kolahal 0.5.9, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.8 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.9.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.9-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **Search the Commands page.** Settings → Commands has a search field at the top. Type a few letters to find a command by its name, by what it does or by another name for it: `youtube` finds `yt`.
- **Widget icons follow the theme.** A widget's icon can be a name, such as `icon = "sun"`: Kolahal draws a line icon in the card's colour, where an emoji keeps its own colours. There are 62 names, for weather, time, travel, money and more; the scripting guide lists them. The library widgets and Kolahal's own cards use them, and a Shortcuts button can too. An emoji or a letter still works.
- **Lists show more.** A row you can tap is a little lower, so a 4×2 card shows three headlines, todos or releases. Buttons and switches keep their size.
- **The Reminders card shows the next one big**: "42 min", with what it is and when under it, and the next few under that. A script's list can have such a line too, with `lead = { value, unit, label }`.
- **`reddit` shows pictures.** A post with a picture shows it under its line, about 640 pixels wide; NSFW and spoiler posts never do. Its `images` option turns them off to save data.
- **A Material You theme.** Kolahal takes its colours from your wallpaper, like the phone's own screens. Pick the **Material You** card in **Settings → Look & feel**, or run `theme material-you`.
  - It changes when the wallpaper does, and it follows dark mode like the other themes.
  - Errors stay red, and the good and warning colours of scripts stay green and amber, so they keep their meaning with every wallpaper.
  - It needs Android 12 or newer. Android 9 to 11 have no wallpaper colours: there the theme shows Material's standard colours, and its card says "Android 12+".
  - `wallpaper match` doesn't work with this theme. The theme follows the wallpaper, so the wallpaper can't follow the theme.
- **A Nord theme.** The colours of [Nord](https://www.nordtheme.com/), with a dark and a light version like the other themes. Pick the **Nord** card, or run `theme nord`.
  - If you made a theme called `nord` or `material-you` yourself, it is now called `nord-2` or `material-you-2`. It keeps its colours, and it stays in use if it was.
- **Find a script in the library.** Scripts → Add script → From the library has a search field at the top, and a row of chips under it.
  - Type a few letters to find a script by its name, its title or its description: `clock` finds World clocks.
  - A chip shows one kind of script: Template, Widget, Command, Timer, Event, Internet or Offline. Its number is how many scripts it shows. With several chips on, the list shows the scripts of any of them.
  - The search and the chips work together: the list shows the scripts that the search finds among those the chips show.
