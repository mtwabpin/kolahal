Kolahal 0.4.15, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.4.14 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.4.15.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.4.15-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **Star a log line to keep it**: long-press a line and pick **Star line**. A starred line looks the same, but it isn't deleted when it's older than `log.keep-days` or past `log.keep-lines`, and `clear` takes it off the screen without deleting it. `/@starred` finds your starred lines (`/@starred wifi` finds the ones with "wifi"), and `log only starred` shows only them. **Unstar line** puts a line back under the usual limits.

**kolahal-0.4.15-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
