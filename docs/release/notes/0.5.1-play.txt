A new Scripts screen: filter scripts by status, how they run, where they came from and more, and sort them by name, last run or recent change. Scripts you edited show Reset, and ones with a new version show Update, right on their card, with Undo. Resets keep your timers and settings. Library scripts you never edited now always update with Kolahal, even old copies. An app update in the log now ends with an up arrow.
