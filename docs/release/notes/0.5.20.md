Kolahal 0.5.20, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.19 and keeps your data and settings.

This release has no APKs: while Kolahal is in testing on Google Play, you install and update it from there. That is the Play build: no Assistant, no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts.

## What's new

- **Undo in Arrange widgets.** After **Close gaps**, a move or a resize, a bar says what happened ("Gaps closed", "Widget moved", "Widget resized") with **Undo**, which puts the whole page back as it was before that change. It goes one change back.
- **Screen time says how much is left.** With a daily limit set (`config set usage.screen-time-limit 180`), the Screen time card's label is the time left ("1h 12m left"), and once you are past it, by how much ("22m over"). A 2×2 card in the Plain or Tonal look also names the app you used most today, with its time.
- **A new app icon.** The log's bar, two lines and the prompt over a dusk scene, the drawing the website has had since November. With themed icons on (Android 13 and newer) it is the log's shapes in your wallpaper's color.
- **Play a one-off shader where you are choosing it.** On the Shaders screen a tap on a one-off, or its **Play** button, plays it once over that page, with the values you set, so you can try one without going home.
- **A scene behind any card.** On a card's settings page (Arrange widgets, the card, **Settings**), **Scene** under What shows puts a still of one of Kolahal's scene shaders behind the card: the pond, dunes, mountains, night and the others. The card's own color lies over it so the text stays readable. It shows in the Plain, Tonal, Bold, Next, Stack and Now looks; Split, Shape and Poster keep their own ground. On Now playing, the cover shows while there is one. Off until you choose one.
- **One setting for the corners of all widgets.** **Settings → Widgets → Widget corners** (`widgets.corners`): Its own, Square, Slightly rounded, Rounded or Very rounded, for every one of Kolahal's cards at once, with your widgets page shown under the choice as you pick. The Split look's tiles follow it too. A card drawn as one shape in the Shape look keeps its shape, and other apps' widgets keep theirs.
- **Icon names as you type one.** Where a card's icon is typed (a script's `icon` option, the Shortcuts card's Icon box), the icon names show under the box with their icons, and a tap fills it. `script set <script> icon` completes them at the prompt.
- **Swipe along the World clocks card's day strips.** A sideways swipe on the strips of a 4×2 World clocks card moves the line hour by hour, and every place's time follows; before, only a tap moved it. To turn the page, swipe beside or below the strips.
- **Put home's pages in your own order.** **Settings → Widgets → Page order** lists the log, each widgets page and the Assistant's page, and you drag them into the order you swipe through (`home.pages`). Left alone, home is as before. Home and Back still return to home's first page, wherever it sits.
- **Last updated on a card.** A script's card has **Last updated** under What shows on its settings page: the card then says how old its content is ("Updated 3 min ago"), so you can tell how fresh a score, a price or the weather is. The live games cards (Cricket, Football) show it at 4×1 in every look. Off until you turn it on; where a size has no room, the page says so.
- **Choose what a swipe on a log line does.** **Settings → Log → Swipes** sets the swipe to the left and the swipe to the right, each to Copy, Delete, Star, Fresh, Run again or None (`log.swipe-left`, `log.swipe-right`). They start as they were: one copies, one deletes. With None a line doesn't move that way, and the swipe turns the page.
- **Fresh from here.** Hold a line and pick **Fresh**: the lines above it are hidden and it becomes the first one shown, as `fresh` does for the whole screen. Nothing is deleted, and "▴ N earlier lines" brings them back.
- **Scripts can open a web link.** `web.open("https://example.org")` opens an `http` or `https` link from a run you started: a command, `script run`, or a tap on the script's widget, button or line. A timer or event run opens nothing. It needs the script's internet access, and a script that uses it asks for `@requires 0.5.20`.

## Changed

- **A shorter menu when you hold a line.** The sheet is about half as tall. What every line has is a row of icons with a word under each, always in the same place: Copy, Fresh, Star, Only this, Delete. Under it, what belongs to that kind of line (a command, a script, an app, a notification, a person, a link, a todo) is a group of small buttons under its own heading. Holding an app in a list shows Open, App info, Favorite, Hide and Uninstall, then its shortcuts.
- **The Agenda's days are chosen on its settings page.** The choice (Auto, 1, 3, 7 or 14 days) moved from the Arrange widgets toolbar to the Agenda card's own settings page. `widgets.agenda-days` is unchanged.
- **A card with old content says "Updated 2 h ago".** It said "2 h ago".
- **`widgets.page` is now `widgets.show`.** The side of the widgets pages (left or right) is no longer a setting: the page order places every page. What is left is a switch, **Show the widgets pages** (`widgets.show`). If you had the widgets on the right or off, your home is carried over as it was, also from an older backup.

## Fixed

- **Bold and Shape charts show the whole week.** The older bars of Unlocks, Screen time and Habit were almost the card's own color; they are the bar's color at 40% now, as in the Plain look.
- **Small colored text is readable in light themes.** A stat's change ("+2.4%"), the agenda's day headings and a Bold list's title are a darker tone of the card's color where the color itself would be faint.
- **Two todos on a small Shape card are easier to hit.** Their rows are spread over the card, so each takes a tap over 46 dp and a near miss no longer ticks the other one.
- **A private space's widget is not named.** An entry under **Restored from backup** reads "Private space widget", and the widgets page shows "[Widget unavailable]" without the app, also when Android hides the locked space altogether.
