Kolahal 0.5.15, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.14 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.15.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.15-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **One app over the months.** `usage-summary month youtube` (or `week`, `year`) shows one app: its time a day, its total, its share of all your screen time, a row for each month with its total and time a day, its change, and when you last used it.
- **Screen time by month, for scripts and widgets.** A script with `@uses usage` reads the same numbers with `usage.summary("month")` (or `"week"`, `"year"`, and a package for one app). A new library script, **screen-time-months**, shows your screen time a day as a bar for each month.
- **Widgets anywhere on the page.** Long-press a widget in Arrange widgets and drag it: it can sit on the right with space beside it, or lower down with a gap above it. Widgets in the way move down, and **Close gaps** packs the page again. Drag the handles on a widget's edges to resize it. Your page looks the same after the update until you move something.
- **Five looks for Kolahal's widgets and scripts' widgets.** Plain, Tonal, Bold, Split and Shape, chosen for each widget with one tap in Arrange widgets, beside one-tap sizes from 1×1 to 4×4. Two weather widgets can look different. Other apps' widgets draw themselves and keep their look. Only the looks that change a card are offered for it, and the script editor's reference can draw its widget examples in each look. Each example there has Copy, its sizes, and variants such as more rows or switches. A single button or switch on a wide card no longer stretches across it.
- **Arrange widgets without an edit mode.** Tap a widget to choose it; a long-press on the widgets page opens the screen with that widget chosen. With TalkBack, each widget has actions to move and resize it.
- **Rosé Pine theme.** `theme rose-pine`, or pick it in Settings → Look & feel: Rosé Pine's own colours in the dark version, and Rosé Pine Dawn in the light one, with its accents a little darker so they read on the light background.
