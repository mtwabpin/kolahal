Kolahal 0.5.15, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.14 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.15.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.15-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **Widgets anywhere on the page.** In Arrange widgets, long-press a widget and drag it: it can sit on the right with space beside it, or lower down with a gap above it. Widgets in the way move down, and **Close gaps** packs the page again. Drag the handles on a widget's edges to resize it. Your page looks the same after the update until you move something.
- **Five looks for Kolahal's widgets and scripts' widgets.** Plain, Tonal, Bold, Split and Shape, chosen for each widget with one tap in Arrange widgets, beside one-tap sizes from 1×1 to 4×4. Two weather widgets can look different. Only the looks that change a card are offered for it; other apps' widgets draw themselves.
- **Arrange widgets without an edit mode.** Tap a widget to choose it, and the toolbar has its look and size. A long-press on the widgets page opens the screen with that widget chosen. With TalkBack, each widget has actions to move and resize it.
- **Now playing moves.** While a track plays, the bar under the header's now-playing line and the Now playing widget's bar are a moving wave, as media players draw them; paused, a flat line.
- **The script reference is a page of its own.** One search for everything, and tabs for functions, directives, widgets, log lines and icons. Each widget example opens its own view: its variants (more rows, more switches, another style), its sizes and its looks, one tap each, with Copy and Insert.
- **Script repositories of your own.** Get more scripts → Sources adds any public repository that publishes a script list, by `owner/name` or by a link to its `index.json`; kolahal-scripts stays first. Start one from [kolahal-scripts-template](https://github.com/mtwabpin/kolahal-scripts-template), and add and update scripts whenever you like. Kolahal still checks each file against the list, shows which repository a script is from, and says that it doesn't check scripts from repositories you add. From the prompt: `script sources`, `script source add`, `script source rm`, and `script get <source>/<name>`.
- **Rosé Pine theme.** `theme rose-pine`, or pick it in Settings → Look & feel: Rosé Pine's colours in the dark version, and Rosé Pine Dawn in the light one, with its accents a little darker so they read on the light background.
- **One app over the months.** `usage-summary month youtube` (or `week`, `year`) shows one app: its time a day, its total, its share of all your screen time, a row for each month with its total and time a day, its change, and when you last used it.
- **Screen time by month, for scripts and widgets.** A script with `@uses usage` reads the same numbers with `usage.summary("month")` (or `"week"`, `"year"`, and a package for one app). A new library script, **screen-time-months**, shows your screen time a day as a bar for each month.
- **Back returns to the widgets page.** Back from a screen opened on the widgets page, such as Arrange widgets, now returns to the widgets page instead of the log.
- **Widgets keep their look when the page shows.** A script's widget no longer appears plain for a moment before taking its look, and one whose content counts as old after a minute (Quick toggles) no longer turns plain until it is filled again.
- **One button or switch doesn't stretch.** A single button or switch on a wide card stays a normal width, in the middle.
- **The Agenda widget looks further ahead.** By default it now reads up to 14 days and shows as many events as the card fits, so a quiet few days no longer leave it empty. To keep it to a few days, tap it in Arrange widgets and choose 1, 3, 7 or 14 under Days, or set Settings → Widgets → Agenda days. A script's `agenda` widget can also show up to 14 days.
- **Labels start with a capital.** Widgets, the script reference, Get more scripts and the editor's bars now start their labels with a capital, as the rest of Kolahal does. Kolahal does this itself for every script's widget and for `fmt.stats`, so a script that writes `label = "steps today"` shows "Steps today", and cards already on the page change at once.
