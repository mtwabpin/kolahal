Kolahal 0.5.19, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.18 and keeps your data and settings.

This release has no APKs: while Kolahal is in testing on Google Play, you install and update it from there. That is the Play build: no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts.

## What's new

- **The Assistant (full build only for now).** A page after the widgets pages to chat with Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT, with your own API key for the service. `config set assistant.page on` shows it; **Settings → Assistant** has the service, the model, the keys, custom instructions and the history. Answers are drawn as cards, lists and rows (A2UI), or come as plain text with **Rich answers** off. Conversations are kept on the phone, the newest 50, and never in a backup; a key is sealed on the phone and sent only to its own service. Nothing else from the phone is sent, and nothing in an answer can run a command, open a link or load a picture.
- **The screen lock, on Google Play too.** `lock`, a double-tap and the Lock screen quick action now work in the Play build. Android lets an app lock the screen only through an accessibility service: before its settings open, Kolahal says what the service is for, that it reads nothing and sends nothing, and that it stays off until you turn it on (`allow lock`).
- **A locked private space shows nothing of what is in it.** Its apps are in no list, selector or suggestion while it is locked, and `apps` has one line that it is locked, with the tap that unlocks it. On Android 16 and newer, Android's own setting to hide the private space when it is locked is followed: then nothing shows, and `private on` still unlocks it.

## Fixed

- **Typing opens the copy of an app that is on.** With an app in a locked private space or a paused work profile as well, typing its name could bring up the unlock question instead of opening the copy you can use.
- **Private space: messages give the real reason.** A shortcut of a locked app said the app had no shortcuts; a Kolahal that is not the home app said the phone had no private space. Every text now says private space, locked and unlocked.
