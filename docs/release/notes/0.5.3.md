Kolahal 0.5.3, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.2 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.3.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.3-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## New

- **Templates in Lua and in JavaScript**: each template now comes in both languages. The JavaScript one ends in `-js`: `template-command-js`, `template-timer-js`, `template-event-js`, `template-api-key-js`, and `template-fetch` is the Lua version of `template-js`.
- **`template-logs`** (and `template-logs-js`): every kind of line a script can log, each next to the code that makes it. Run it and read its numbered lines beside its code: bodies, links and Copy text, styles and colours, tags, Markdown, lists, tables, bars, big numbers, a picture, a list with space between its items, buttons, a line that `line.set` updates, and the status line.

## Changed

- **Starting on the widgets page needs the app selector** (`widgets.start` needs `selector.rail`): there it is your way to open apps. Settings shows the option only while the selector is on, and `config set widgets.start on` asks you to turn the selector on first. If you turn the selector off, home opens on the log; turn it back on and home opens on the widgets page again.
- **`usage` shows app names on one line**, ending with “…” when too long. An app Kolahal can't name, such as the phone's in-call screen (`com.samsung.android.incallui`), no longer shows as its package: its time counts in an "Other apps" line at the end, with the apps past the first 12, so the times add up to the total.

## Fixed

- **The app selector on the widgets page**: while its list is open, the widgets fade out, as the log does on its page. Before, a dim layer covered only part of the page and left bright bands above and below it.
- **A caller's picture on the call line**: the line logged when a call ends now shows the caller's photo whenever the contact has one. Before, it often had none, because the phone app hadn't copied the photo into the call log yet, and some never do.

**kolahal-0.5.3-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
