Kolahal 0.5.16, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.15 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.16.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.16-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **World clocks say how far each place is.** Under each place the card shows its difference from your phone's time: "5 h 30 min behind", "3 h ahead", "Same time", or "Here", with the day when it differs. At 4×2, each place also gets a strip of your day shaded by its night, day and working hours: tap a strip to see every clock at that hour, and tap **Now** to come back.
- **A month of dots for your habit.** The habit widget shows a dot for each day, a row for each week: filled for a day done, a ring for a day missed, so the weekday you tend to skip stands out. A new habit starts with today's dot alone. Scripts can draw the same with `style = "dots"` on a chart.
- **Each day's range on one scale.** A list row can have `range = { low, high }`, drawn as a bar on a scale shared by the rows, with an optional `value` marked by a dot. The forecast widget uses it, so the cooler day shows at a glance.
- **Now playing shows the cover, seeks, and remembers.** The album cover fills the play and pause button, and you can drag the bar, at any size, to move within the track. With nothing playing, it offers the app that played last, with **Play** to start it again.
- **Cards without a title line.** Plain and Tonal cards no longer have a title line over their content, as Bold, Split and Shape never had: the content gets the room. A list or a text shows its name small over itself, and a number, bar, ring, chart or countdown with no label uses the name as its label. On a large card, a script's note ("Done today") and how old the content is ("2 h ago", "Failed 10:42") are the last line.
- **Undo for a habit.** Once today is done, a tap on the habit widget takes the mark back, so a tap by mistake is easy to fix. The streak and the dots come back as they were.
- **A daily counter.** The new library script **counter** counts anything through the day, glasses of water to start with. Tap the count to add one, and **Undo** to take one back. With a daily target it shows "5 of 8" and turns green once you reach it; without one, today's count alone. Each day starts at 0, and each placed widget keeps its own count.
- **The script reference names each widget's kind.** Every widget example has its `kind`, in Lua and JavaScript, and the reference lists what it left out, such as `usage.data("month")`, a list's `lead` and the words `trend` takes.
