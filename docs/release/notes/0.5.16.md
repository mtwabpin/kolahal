Kolahal 0.5.16, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.15 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.16.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.16-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **World clocks say how far each place is.** Under each place the card shows its difference from your phone's time: "5 h 30 min behind", "3 h ahead", "Same time", or "Here", with the day when it differs. At 4×2, each place also gets a strip of your day shaded by its night, day and working hours: tap a strip to see every clock at that hour, and tap **Now** to come back.
- **A month of dots for your habit.** The habit widget shows a dot for each day, a row for each week: filled for a day done, a ring for a day missed, so the weekday you tend to skip stands out. A new habit starts with today's dot alone. Scripts can draw the same with `style = "dots"` on a chart.
- **Each day's range on one scale.** A list row can have `range = { low, high }`, drawn as a bar on a scale shared by the rows, with an optional `value` marked by a dot. The forecast widget uses it, so the cooler day shows at a glance.
- **Now playing shows the cover, seeks, and remembers.** The album cover fills the play and pause button, and you can drag the bar, at any size, to move within the track. With nothing playing, it offers the app that played last, with **Play** to start it again. A long-press on the header's music line offers Next track, Previous track and Open the player.
- **Cards without a title line.** Plain and Tonal cards no longer have a title line over their content, as Bold, Split and Shape never had: the content gets the room. A list or a text shows its name small over itself, and a number, bar, ring, chart or countdown with no label uses the name as its label. On a large card, a script's note ("Done today") and how old the content is ("2 h ago", "Failed 10:42") are the last line.
- **Undo for a habit.** Once today is done, a tap on the habit widget takes the mark back, so a tap by mistake is easy to fix. The streak and the dots come back as they were.
- **A daily counter.** The new library script **counter** counts anything through the day, glasses of water to start with. Tap the count to add one, and **Undo** to take one back. With a daily target it shows "5 of 8" and turns green once you reach it; without one, today's count alone. Each day starts at 0, and each placed widget keeps its own count.
- **The widgets page without a swipe.** `widgets page` turns home to the widgets page, and so does a **Widgets page** quick action while the page is on (`widgets` still opens Arrange widgets). With TalkBack or Switch Access, home has **Widgets** and **Log** actions.
- **The pinned line by hand.** A tap on the dots of the line pinned at the top of home shows its next item. With a screen reader on, the items no longer take turns by themselves.
- **Run with… from the script's sheet.** A script's sheet has **Run with…** for arguments or an event, and after a plain Run the editor says that a long-press on Run does the same.
- **Undo for resets and Mark all read.** Reset to default says "Reset" with **Undo**, and has a larger touch area. Mark all read on a feed says how many it marked, with **Undo**.
- **Searches find words in any order.** Settings and the widget picker find "bar color" as well as "color bar", and Settings also finds a choice by its name.
- **The script reference keeps its place.** It reopens where you left it (search, tab, group and scroll) while the editor is open, for each script.
- **The script reference names each widget's kind.** Every widget example has its `kind`, in Lua and JavaScript, and fits a phone's width in one style. The reference also lists what it left out before, such as `usage.data("month")`, a list's `lead` and the words `trend` takes.
- **American English.** Kolahal's own words are now in American English: color, favorite, canceled. A setting or command named this way is the same setting or command, and what you saved stays as it was.

## Fixed

- **Phones without app widgets.** Kolahal no longer fails to start on a phone without other apps' widgets, such as Android Go. Kolahal's own widgets work there, and the widget picker says the phone doesn't support other apps' widgets.
- **A restore to a new phone keeps your widgets.** After Android restores Kolahal on a new phone, every other app's widget waits on Arrange widgets for **Add again**, in its place, instead of being removed as "uninstalled" when its app wasn't back yet.
- **Other apps' widgets follow dark mode.** When dark mode turns on or off, or a new wallpaper brings new colors, other apps' widgets are drawn again in the new colors.
- **Split cards with nothing to show.** A card with nothing to show yet ("Nothing playing", an empty list, loading) keeps its card in the Split look, instead of a faint line straight on the wallpaper.
- **Resizing goes back.** In Arrange widgets, a resize handle dragged too far and back no longer leaves the widgets beside it pushed down.
- **Quick toggles stay still.** A large switches card says how old its states are only after an hour, so "3 min ago" no longer comes and goes on most visits.
- **A long link on a card.** A tap link longer than 2000 characters is now an error from `widget.show`, instead of a card that later says "Widget unavailable".
- **A swipe never deletes by accident.** With the widgets page on the right, swiping a log line left copies it and right deletes it, so a swipe towards the page never deletes; a copy says "Copied". In a right-to-left language the sides swap with the page.
- **A quick second tap counts.** A second tap on a widget's button within 2 seconds now runs when the 2 seconds end, so two quick taps on the counter add two.
- **Nothing typed is lost.** A text setting, and a script's Daily at, take what you typed when the field loses focus or the sheet closes; a value that isn't valid stays marked.
- **Back in the theme builder asks first.** Once a color or the name changed, Back asks "Discard changes?".
- **A near miss stays on the widgets page.** On the Todos and Shortcuts cards, only the list's name or "+3 more" opens the list, and the gaps between shortcuts do nothing.
- **TalkBack.** The music line says "Pause" or "Play", `$` says "Quick actions", and the Undo row is announced.
- **Feeds.** A database error on the Feeds screen no longer closes home, and the screens say "1 new feed" and "1 feed".
