Kolahal 0.5.12, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.11 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.12.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.12-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **One app's screen time.** `usage whatsapp` shows one app: its time today, how often you opened it, its longest session and when you last used it, a column for each hour today, and the last 7 days with their average.
- **Days with no data are left out.** Android keeps usage data for the last days only, about 10 on many phones. `unlocks 14` and `usage 14` now leave out the days before that, and average over the days that are there: before, such a day counted as 0. Kolahal also saves each day's unlock count from now on, so `unlocks` goes back further over time. `device.unlocks(days)` in a script leaves those days out too.
