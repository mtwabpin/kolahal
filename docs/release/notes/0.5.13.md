Kolahal 0.5.13, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.12 and keeps your data and settings.

- **kolahal-0.5.13.apk**: the full build.
- **kolahal-0.5.13-play.apk**: the Play build, the same app as on Google Play: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Play Protect may block either APK when sideloaded in India and some other countries, as both have notification access: install with scanning off, as the install guide says. Both APKs use the same package and key, so either installs over the other.

## What's new

- **The long view of your screen time.** Android keeps coarser totals besides its days: about 4 weeks, 6 months and 2 years. `usage-summary` shows a line for each, with the screen time a day. `usage-summary week`, `month` and `year` show a row for each week, month or year with its dates, its total and its time a day, compare the last one with those before, and list the top apps; `usage-summary year` puts the years side by side, with the apps you use less or that are new. `usage-summary full` shows all of them. It keeps to numbers and short labels: `≈` marks Android's own totals, and `help usage-summary` explains the rest.
- **Removed and reinstalled apps.** When you uninstall or reinstall an app, Android deletes its history from some of its levels and not others. `usage-summary` now counts each app in a year at least as much as the months inside it hold, so a reinstalled app no longer makes the year look shorter than its months. An app you removed shows by its package name with a *removed* chip, while Android still has time for it.
- **Fewer words in usage output.** `usage <app>`, `usage <days>`, `unlocks` and `data month` drop their long sentences: `2h 24m a day · 6 days`, `9 of 14 days`, `▼ 12% · last 7 days`, `No plan · config set data.plan 75`, and a `── by week ──` line. The **data-month** widget script says "No plan" the same way.
- **Iosevka.** A fifth bundled terminal font, narrow, so more fits on a line: `font iosevka`, or pick it in Settings → Look & feel.
