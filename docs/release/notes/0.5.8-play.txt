Widgets from scripts:
• A script can fill a card on the widgets page: a number, a list, a chart, a countdown, a clock, a picture, buttons or switches.
• Six cards of Kolahal's own: Todos, Reminders, Notes, Shortcuts, Now playing and Agenda.
• 18 library scripts have a widget: weather, crypto, live scores, habit streaks and more.
• The same widget can be placed more than once, each with its own options.
• Scripts can read your todos, notes, calendar and more, each only if you allow it.
