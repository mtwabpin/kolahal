Kolahal 0.5.22, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.21 and keeps your data and settings.

This release has no APKs: while Kolahal is in testing on Google Play, you install and update it from there. That is the Play build: no Assistant, no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts.

## What's new

- **Six more fields for script widgets.** A stat can show its target ("6,240 of 8,000", with a mark past 100%), a button or switch a value such as "21°", a progress card a line under each stage, a versus card a line under each side, any card but buttons and switches a small badge such as "live" or "3", and any card a footer for a source or note. Each is optional and gives way where a card has no room. A script that uses one asks for `@requires 0.5.22`.

## Fixed

- **A notice no longer moves the log.** "Line deleted" with its Undo, "Screen cleared", "Copied" and "Starred" showed in a row that pushed every line up and let it drop back. The notice floats over the bottom of the log now, and a tap or swipe beside its Undo reaches the line under it.
