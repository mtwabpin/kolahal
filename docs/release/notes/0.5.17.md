Kolahal 0.5.17, package `com.mtwabpin.kolahal`, Android 9 or newer. It installs over 0.5.16 and keeps your data and settings.

This release has no APKs: while Kolahal is in testing on Google Play, you install and update it from there. That is the Play build: no screen lock (`lock`, double-tap), no calls in the log and no `update`, and it finds people with Android's contact picker instead of reading your contacts. Everything in these notes is in it, but the fix for calls in the log.

## What's new

- **Shaders over home.** On Android 13 and newer, an effect can be drawn over home and the full-screen screens: 45 come with Kolahal, from an old CRT, e-paper and graph paper to rain, snowfall, mountains and an aurora. Tap one on the **Shaders** tab, beside Settings, Scripts and Feeds (`shaders` opens it), or type `shader rain`. `shader off`, or two fingers held still on the screen for a second, always turns it off. Pictures, icons and emoji keep their colors under a scene; only text gets more contrast, and Lamp darkens text and pictures a little away from its light, on purpose. A scene brings its own ground: the phone's wallpaper doesn't show through the log while it is on, and comes back when the shader is off or is one that works on the text.
- **Scenes of how you use the phone.** Eight shaders draw your screen time and unlocks: `candle` and `hourglass` run down as today's screen time is spent, `seismograph` and `day-ring` trace today, and `windows`, `record`, `lanterns` and `kintsugi` show the week. Screen time needs usage access (`allow usage`). A shader can't send or save anything: it only draws.
- **Shaders that follow your day.** Sliders in a shader's details change it, such as the amount of snow (`shader set snowfall amount 1.5`), or let it follow the battery, the time of day, your unlocks or a value a script gives. With the library's **weather** script, `shader follow rain amount weather.rain` makes it rain on home as it rains outside.
- **One-off shaders.** Fourteen shaders play once over home for a few seconds, drawn over everything on the screen, a scene included. `hearts` comes with six more ways for hearts to go: a fountain from the bottom (`hearts-fountain`), petals falling (`hearts-falling`), a stream up the right edge (`hearts-stream`), poppers from the corners (`hearts-poppers`), fireworks (`hearts-fireworks`) and hearts that bounce in a row (`hearts-bounce`). `pulse`, light that runs around the edge, comes with six more made of light: a comet around the edge (`comet`), the edge breathing (`breath`), a scan line (`scan-line`), light spilling from the top (`spill`), a heartbeat trace (`trace`) and northern lights (`northern-lights`). Type `shader play hearts`, or set words under **Plays on a notification** in its details, and it plays when a notification has one of them. The hearts come in eight styles, under **Heart** in the details or with `shader set hearts style neon`: glossy, flat, gradient, outline, double, neon, cartoon or halftone (`hearts-fireworks` draws sparks and has none).
- **One-off shaders over the app you are in.** Turn on **Play over other apps** on the **Shaders** tab (`screen.shader-over-apps`) and allow Android's Display over other apps (`allow overlay`), and a notification with one of a shader's words plays it at once over the app in front. Before Android's page opens, Kolahal says what it will draw and when. Every one-off that comes with Kolahal can play there; a shader of your own says so in its header. Touches go through to the app, and nothing of the app is read. The shader waits for home while the phone is locked, when Do Not Disturb holds the notification back, over a full-screen app, in battery saver and with a screen reader on. Android Go and other low-memory phones can't allow Display over other apps, so Kolahal doesn't offer it there.
- **A shader as your wallpaper.** **Set as wallpaper** in a shader's details, or `wallpaper shader pond`, opens Android's own preview with it, where you make it the live wallpaper of the home screen, the lock screen or both, behind any launcher. `wallpaper` says what the wallpaper is now and lists what it can become, and `wallpaper live` opens Android's own list of live wallpapers, for a phone whose wallpaper settings no longer link to it.
- **Shaders of your own.** **Shaders → Add shader** imports `.agsl` files or starts from one of six templates with comments, and **Edit** in a shader's details (`shader edit <name>`) opens it in the script editor, where **Save** says whether it compiles. **Get more shaders** (`shader get`) lists the ones people share. In a shader's header, `@fps` and `@settles` keep a moving one from being drawn more often than it needs, and a choice can have up to eight words, shown in two rows. While a shader reads home, the page is drawn a step under opaque, so a pixel at full alpha (`a > 0.999`) is text or a picture.
- **More than one widgets page.** Widgets can go on up to five pages beside the terminal. Add, name and remove pages in Arrange widgets, move a widget with the toolbar's Page row or by holding it at the screen's edge, and turn home to a page with `widget page 2` or `widget page work`. `widget ls` lists widgets under each page's name, and a backup keeps your pages and their names; older backups put everything on page 1.
- **A settings page for each card.** **Settings** in Arrange widgets opens a page for the chosen card, in place of the Options sheet: the card itself at the top, changing as you choose, then its own options (a weather card's city), its color (any of the theme's colors, the card's own first), its shape in the Shape look (fifteen Material shapes, from cookies and clovers to an arch, a ghost and a pill), what shows (its name and its icon), and a reset to the card's own, which **Undo** takes back. Colors a script gives to parts of a card, such as a gauge's zones, stay, and a script can name the card's own shape with `shape`. The choices are kept for each placed card and go into backups.
- **New widget cards.** Two new kinds for scripts: `versus` shows two sides and the score between them, each with a lettered mark, a two-part bar such as chess clocks or possession, a dot on the side to move and a last line (`caption`); `sky` shows the sun on its arc from sunrise to sunset, or the moon in its phase with its next phases, and Kolahal moves it as time passes with no run of the script. A progress widget can be a gauge (`style = "gauge"`: a thick arc in the colors of its `zones`, with the value inside), rings inside each other (`style = "rings"`), or stages on a line for a parcel or a flight (`stages` and `stage`). A number widget can show its recent values as a soft line behind it (`points`) or have one of Kolahal's own scenes behind it (`scene = "pond"`), which follows the time of day with no run, and its digits roll to a new value. A countdown has two more styles: `dots` shows the days left as dots, one going out each day, and `page` shows the date as a calendar page.
- **A new look, and shapes that say more.** **Poster** sets a number, a countdown or a clock so large that the card's edge crops it, with the label small in a corner. In **Shape**, a picture is cut to a shape, a countdown is a sun that loses a ray as each day goes (each hour on its last day), a week of a habit is a flower with a leaf for each day done, and the battery goes from round when full to spiky when low. Shapes that change settle with a small overshoot, and a held button widens while the buttons beside it give way.
- **Cards with a look of their own.** Todos has **Next**: the next todo large with a check to tap, a ring that fills as todos are ticked, and Undo for the last tick. Reminders has **Next** too: the next reminder in a pill that empties over its last hour, with the later ones as points on a line. Notes has **Stack**: the newest note on a stack of sheets, and + to start a new note in one tap. The agenda has **Now**, on Kolahal's card and on scripts' agenda cards: what is on now and how far along it is, the free time until the next event ("1 h 30 min", as the other cards write a length), and what comes next; a large card shows the day with the free time between events.
- **Widget settings for other apps' widgets.** In Arrange widgets, **Widget settings** opens a widget's setup again, to pick another city or calendar without removing it, when its app allows that.
- **Daily limits.** Set a limit for screen time and one for unlocks in **Settings → Header → Daily limits**, or with `config set usage.screen-time-limit 180` (minutes) and `config set usage.unlock-limit 60`. At the limit, today's figure turns the theme's warning color, and from one and a half times the limit its error color: in the header's unlocks, `usage`, `unlocks`, `top` and `fetch`, with a line that says how far over it is. Scripts read the screen time limit and its color from `usage.today`, and the library's Screen time card and `tty-top` take that color.
- **Library scripts on the new cards.** `football` and `cricket` show the score on a versus card, `aqi` draws its index as a gauge with the scale's zones, and `sun` has a widget: the sky card with the day's sunrise and sunset, or the day's length where the sun doesn't rise or set.
- **Home on a character grid.** `config set home.style tty` draws home's log, header and prompt on a strict grid, like a terminal: one font size and no pictures. `tty.suggestions`, `tty.selector`, `tty.keybar`, `tty.quick-actions` and `tty.menu` put the other parts on it too, and `config set home.style gui` goes back.
- **Full-screen screens and a screensaver.** With `home.style tty`, `matrix`, `pipes`, `sl`, `clock`, `top`, `decode` and `boot` take the whole screen until a tap, Back or a key; `screen <name>` opens one in either home style. **Settings → Look & feel → Screensaver** opens `matrix`, `clock`, `pipes` or `decode` over home after 30 seconds to 60 minutes without a touch (`screen.saver`, `screen.saver-after`); in the default home style it offers `decode`, which scrambles home's own lines where they are.
- **`scramble` for screen recordings.** With it on, home's log keeps each line's place, length and colors but no real text, and pictures show as plain blocks, until `scramble off`. Nothing stored changes, and a tap works on the real line. The header's time, date, battery and counts, what you type and the other screens stay readable.
- **For scripts.** Type `skill.md` in full to copy a guide for an AI assistant on writing Kolahal scripts and shaders. Scripts can draw a full-screen screen of their own (`tty.screen`), give shaders a value to follow (`shader.give`) and play a one-off shader (`shader.play`). The library's `template-screen`, in Lua and in JavaScript, is a screen to start your own from, and its `tty-` scripts, such as `tty-matrix`, draw Kolahal's own screens as scripts. In the default home style a script that draws a screen is marked **TTY style only** in Scripts and in the library, and home doesn't suggest it; its name typed in full still runs it. Kolahal asks each source of shared scripts and shaders for the list of its own version first, and for the general list when the source has none, so scripts for a new version can be shared before it is released.
- **The editor's reference shows more.** It names every widget kind, style and field, each with an example: a stat's scene and bar, list checkboxes, a gauge from below zero, single bars and rings, a chart on a fixed scale, a countdown on its day, a clock with seconds, switches with icons and sides in team colors. A log example can be seen in either home style: **Home style** under its preview switches between GUI and TTY.
- **Messages that fit a phone.** What Kolahal writes in the log says what happened on its first line, and what to do about it on the lines under it, where a long line used to wrap. When a message suggests a command, **Copy text** on that line copies the command. An error can have such lines too. The descriptions of settings are shorter sentences.
- **`apps` at a glance.** `apps` shows each app's icon on its chip, in three even columns, and a profile with many apps shows its pinned and most-used apps first, then "+N more", which opens the rest in place. Each profile's heading says how many apps it has ("── work · 18 ──"), a single profile gets one count line, and the legend line at the end is gone. The icons follow **Show app icons in suggestions**. In the app selector, work and private apps show the system's badge on their icon in place of the `[W]`/`[S]` tag; the tag comes back with icons off and in the TTY style, and a screen reader hears the profile in every case.
- **The home app, from About.** **Settings → About → Default home app** says whether Kolahal is your home app. A tap makes it the home app with Android's own dialog, or, once it is, opens Android's page where you choose another.
- **Smaller changes.** On Android 12 and newer, the header and the prompt keep clear of the screen's rounded corners. Where an option needs an access that Android hasn't given, such as the daily limits (usage access) or a one-off's words (notification access), a line and an **Allow** button stand in its place. In Settings, tap and hold a number setting, or a shader's parameter, to type its value. At the prompt, `config set log.time ` and other choice settings show a few words beside their values. In Scripts, a sort other than by name shows as a chip that one tap clears, and names sort by the name on the card, whatever its case. The script library lists its templates last, under **Templates**. In the Shape look, a clock card's place name moves below the center while the hour hand points at it, and has a ring in the card's color so it reads over the minute hand. The first-run tour turns the app selector on when its page opens, so you can try it at once; Off on that page stays off, also when you run `tour` again. In the TTY style, a row of big numbers that is slightly too wide cuts its longest label with "…" so the row stays whole: weather's four figures fit on one row on a phone.
- **A quicker start.** After Kolahal starts, the log's lines are ready in about half the time, in time for the first frame, and home does less for what is out of sight.

## Fixed

- **Hidden bars stay hidden.** With **Hide system bars** on, a sheet or a dialog over home no longer brings the status bar back while it is open.
- **Calls show once** (full build). A call that the phone's call log wrote more than once, as some phones do while a call is going on, is one line in the log, updated in place.
- **A crash in lists.** A rare crash ("LayoutNode … not found in RectList") when a list prepared a row ahead of time is fixed.
- **Other apps' widgets fit.** A new widget from another app starts at the width its app asks for, and can't be made narrower than its app allows. A widget dropped near another one's top or bottom lines up with it, and the widgets page keeps its place after Arrange widgets.
- **A backup doesn't bring widgets twice.** Importing a backup no longer offers again the app widgets that are already on the page.
- **Cards at large font sizes.** List and agenda rows grow with the font size, a row's text at the end that doesn't fit ends in "…", and an emoji icon made of several characters stays whole.
- **Cards follow a change at once.** An option changed in a script's sheet or with `script set` runs its placed widgets again. A card that an access switch stopped opens Scripts on a tap, and runs again when you turn the switch on.
- **Clearer card errors.** A failed card names the script and the line once, and a `@widget` line with a kind that doesn't exist says so when it runs.
- **Cards that read right.** A wide number widget's smaller values read in the order the script gives them, and in the Shape look a level's or a progress card's number is drawn in two colors at the fill line, so it reads on both sides in every theme.
- **Importing one script** in Scripts names it ("Off until you turn it on with 'script on chess'"), and Copy on its log line gives that command.
- **A second card keeps to itself.** A second widget of `weather`, `aqi`, `crypto`, `football` or `cricket` with options of its own no longer changes the script's pinned line (a Paris card made a pinned Tokyo line read Paris), a second `football` or `cricket` card follows its own team's match, and a second `habit` card keeps its own days and turns to the new day when it is next seen after midnight. A new second card no longer shows what a removed one stored, such as a habit's days or a followed match: a number a script's card had is never given again, and a backup keeps them.
- **Countdowns** change their date at local midnight, not at midnight UTC.
- **Searches.** Get more scripts and Add widget have a clear button in the search field, and Back there and in the Reference clears a typed search before it leaves, as in Scripts and Settings.
- **The slim seek bar** of a one-row Now playing card takes a finger a little under it too, so it is easier to drag.
- **TalkBack in Arrange widgets** stops once on each card, and another app's widget's own buttons no longer take its focus there.
- **Work profile and private space.** While a private space is locked, its widget names neither its app nor itself: on the widgets page, in `widget ls` and on the widget in Arrange widgets. A long-press on the placeholder of a paused or locked widget opens Arrange widgets.
- **Lines in order.** When a command starts part of its work in the background, its own lines come first: adding a source of scripts could show how many scripts it has above the line that says it was added.

## Changed

- **Rosé Pine is the default theme.** `rose-pine`, with Rosé Pine Dawn as its light version, is the default and comes first in the theme list. If you never chose a theme, Kolahal shows it after this update, and so it does if you had typed `theme green` while green was the default; `theme green` brings the old look back. Before the theme is read, the window shows the theme's background instead of black.
- **New command names.** `screen`, `shader` and `scramble` are now Kolahal's commands, and with `home.style tty` so are `matrix`, `pipes`, `sl`, `clock`, `top`, `decode` and `boot`. A line that starts with one of them runs the command, not an alias, a script or an app of that name. To open your clock app by typing `clock` in the TTY style, turn the command off in **Settings → Commands**. A script of yours with one of these names still runs with `script run <name>`, and `script mv <name> <new-name>` renames it; an alias of `screen`, `shader` or `scramble` needs a new name. In the default home style the screens' names stay yours: `clock` opens your alias, your script or the app, as before.
- **Commands that moved.** Kolahal is in testing, and these changed without keeping their old names. An alias, a gesture or a shortcut of yours that runs an old one needs the new one.
  - **`allow` asks for every Android permission.** `allow` alone lists them, each marked allowed or not; tap one, or type `allow usage`, `allow send-notifications`, `allow read-notifications`, `allow calendar`, `allow contacts`, `allow calls`, `allow dnd`, `allow settings`, `allow alarms`, `allow lock`, `allow battery` or `allow overlay`. Gone: `usage-access`, `notifications`, `accessibility`, `battery`, `notifs enable`, `dnd access`, `brightness access`, `remind exact`, and `--allow` and `--settings` after `agenda`, `calls` and `contacts`. `notifications` is now another name of `notifs`.
  - **`usage` took over `usage-summary`:** `usage summary`, `usage week`, `usage month`, `usage year` (each of the three also with an app) and `usage full`.
  - **`widget` acts and `widgets` opens the screen,** as `script` and `scripts` do: `widget add`, `widget ls` and `widget page`; `widget` alone lists your widgets. `widgets add`, `widgets ls` and `widgets page` work too.
  - **`wallpaper match` is `wallpaper theme`:** a plain wallpaper in your theme's color, so your wallpaper doesn't flash when you swipe home. **Settings → Look & feel → Wallpaper** offers it beside a shader and Android's list of live wallpapers.
  - **In `help` and Settings → Commands,** `theme`, `font`, `shader` and `wallpaper` are together under **Look**.
- **A setting with a clearer name.** `screen.bars` is `screen.hide-bars`, **Hide system bars** in Settings, and its values say what is hidden: `none`, `status` (the status bar) or `both` (the navigation bar too), in place of `show`, `hide` and `immersive`. What you had set carries over.
- **More sizes.** Progress widgets are also offered at 4×1 and 4×2, and picture widgets at 2×1.
- **Todos has no Shape look.** Next took its place there, since Shape drew Todos almost as Plain does; a Todos card that had Shape shows Plain.
- **Buttons take the card's color.** A buttons card's plain buttons are drawn in the card's color, not always the accent.
- **`widget page` waits for Enter,** since a page's number or name can follow it; it used to run as soon as it was typed in full.
- **Whole hours of screen time read "3h".** `usage` and `top` write a whole hour as "3h", not "3h 0m".

**kolahal-0.5.17-mapping.zip** is only for reading crash reports from these builds; you don't need it to install.
