## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Launch apps by typing" icon="seti:shell">
		Any text that isn't a command is matched against app names. Press Enter to launch the highlighted best match. Turn on `suggestions.idle` to see your favourites and most-used apps on an empty prompt.
	</Card>
	<Card title="An interactive log" icon="list-format">
		Every command and its output is a group with a coloured bar. Tap a line for its main action.
		Long-press for an action sheet with copy, share, open, rerun and more.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Scripts in Lua or JS" icon="seti:lua">
		Timers (`every 30m`, `at 08:00`), notifications, status lines you can pin at the top and `@command` scripts that
		take arguments. They run sandboxed in a separate process.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Feeds and notifications" icon="rss">
		Built-in RSS/Atom subscriptions with unread counts. With notification access, new notifications
		show up in the log, each ending in its app's icon.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Built-in tools" icon="puzzle">
		`= 2^10`, `= 5 km in mi`, `= 100 usd in eur`, web search, `call`, `sms`, app shortcuts, screen
		time and an unlock counter.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Work profile and private space" icon="seti:lock">
		If you have them, their apps appear in the same list as your personal apps, tagged `[W]` and `[S]` (`[C]` for
		clones). Pause or resume the work profile from the header or with `work off`.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Themes

`theme green` (the default) · `amber` · `mono` · `dracula` · `solarized` · `gruvbox` · `gruvbox-material`,
each in a dark and a light version that follow the phone's dark mode.
This site uses the green palette in dark mode and gruvbox light in light mode.
