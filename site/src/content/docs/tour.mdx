Each part below is one thing Kolahal does, shown in screenshots of the app. Where there is a command
to try, type it at the prompt. The link at the end of a part goes to the guide's page about it.

## Opening apps by typing

Type any part of an app's name. The best match sits at the bottom, nearest your thumb, and Enter
opens it. When the letters fit only one app, it opens without Enter.

## The app selector

Slide a thumb along the edge of the screen. The list of apps moves with your thumb, and the app level
with it is the one you get. Slide onto the list and lift to open it, or hold still until its row fills.
The first-run tour turns it on, and its page picks the edge or turns it off. **Settings → App selector** does the same later.

## Commands and the log

Sums and units start with `=`. Other commands answer in the same place, the log. Tap a line for its
main action, swipe it right to copy it or left to delete it, and long-press it for all of its actions.
`fresh` gives a clean screen. Nothing is deleted.

<Shots
	items={[
		{ file: 'shots/log/answers.png', alt: 'The log with a sum and a conversion in large type, the todo list, a reminder that was set, and the time in Tokyo and Lisbon', caption: 'Answers in the log.' },
		{ file: 'shots/log/actions.png', alt: 'The action sheet of a feed line: the line, when it arrived, run again, edit, show only commands, star, copy and delete', caption: 'Long-press a line for its actions.' },
		{ file: 'shots/log/fresh.png', alt: 'A clean screen after fresh, with one line above the prompt: 12 earlier lines, tap to show', caption: '<code>fresh</code>: a clean screen.' },
	]}
/>

Try `= 5 km in mi`, `date tokyo london` and `fresh`. More in [The log](/getting-started/basics/#the-log).

Do Not Disturb, has a switch of its own, and a script that never goes online gets no internet.

## A script's line at the top

A script can keep one line of status. Pin it to the top of home: a script never pins itself. A line
can carry a bar that shows how far along it is, and pinned lines take turns.

## Widgets that scripts fill

Scripts fill cards on a page beside home. A tap on a card runs its script again. You place a
script's widget: it never places itself.

## A widget's options, size and look

Long-press a card to arrange it. Each card has its own size, from 1×1 to 4×4, and its own look:
Plain, Tonal, Bold, Split or Shape. A script's options set what it shows, such as a second weather
card for another city.

## Shaders

A shader draws a scene behind home. Some move, some stand still, some answer your touch, and others
change how the text looks. Some are drawn from your screen time and unlocks, which never leave the
phone. Shaders need Android 13 or newer.

<Shots
	items={[
		{ file: 'shots/shaders/list.png', alt: 'Settings, Shaders, searched for screen time: Record, Candle and Hourglass, each with what it draws, and chips for dark theme, light theme, wallpaper and still', caption: 'Settings → Shaders.' },
		{ file: 'shots/shaders/lanterns.png', alt: 'Home over the lanterns scene, a lantern for each day\'s unlocks over dark water, with the week\'s unlocks in the log', caption: '<code>shader lanterns</code>: the week\'s unlocks.' },
		{ file: 'shots/shaders/candle.png', alt: 'Home over the candle scene, a candle burning down with the day\'s screen time, with the day\'s screen time in the log', caption: '<code>shader candle</code>: today\'s screen time.' },
	]}
/>

Try `shader mountains`, and `shader off` to turn it off. More in [Shaders](/scripting/shaders/).

## A shader's parameters

Some shaders have parameters, such as the number of birds over the mountains. Drag a slider and the
preview follows. A parameter can also follow a value: the battery, the time, your screen time or
unlocks, or a script's.

<Shots
	items={[
		{ file: 'shots/shader-parameters/sliders.png', alt: 'The details of the mountains shader: its preview, what it draws, and a slider for the number of birds', caption: 'The birds of <code>mountains</code>.' },

	]}
/>

Try `shader set snowfall amount 1.5`, or `shader follow dunes camels battery / 20`. More in [Turning a shader on](/scripting/shaders/#turning-a-shader-on) and [Values to follow](/scripting/shaders/#values-to-follow).

## And the TTY style

One setting draws home on a character grid, like a terminal: one font size, whole lines, and the
suggestions and the key bar on the grid too. Some commands then take the whole screen, such as `clock`,
`matrix` and `top`.

<Shots
	items={[
		{ file: 'shots/tty/home.png', alt: 'Home drawn on a character grid: the weather, a calendar for the month and the todo list', caption: 'Home on a grid.' },

	]}
/>

Try `config set home.style tty`, and `config set home.style gui` to go back. More in [TTY home style](/reference/config/#tty-home-style).
