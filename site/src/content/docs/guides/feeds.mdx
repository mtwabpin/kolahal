Kolahal has a small feed reader built in. No scripting needed.

## Subscribe

```sh
feed add https://hnrss.org/frontpage
feed add lwn.net/headlines/rss       # https:// is assumed; feed:// links work too
```

Adding a feed fetches it right away. The address is normalised, so adding the same feed twice
returns the existing subscription. `feed://` links open as `https://`. A feed can be on your own
network (a home server), except on Android 17 and newer, where Android blocks it and the fetch fails
after a timeout. Kolahal loads the newest 300 items of a feed.

## Read

<Image src={terminal} alt="Feed items in the log, each with its feed name, and the feed list with unread counts" class="shot shot-single" width={320} />

```sh
feed                 # list: #id, title, unread/total, interval, notify, last status (also: feed ls)
feed read            # all unread items, then mark them read
feed read 2          # unread items of feed #2
feed all 2           # the 40 newest stored items of feed #2, read or not
feed open 2          # open up to 5 unread items of #2 in the browser, mark them read
feed fetch           # refresh every feed now (feed fetch 2 for one)
```

Item lines are tappable links; long-press to share or copy. When a fetch finds new items they
appear in the log, each feed's in a group of its own, at most `feeds.log-lines` (default 10) per
feed, with a "+N more" line (tapping it puts `feed read <id>` in the prompt).

## Read and unread

Every item starts **unread** when it is first fetched. It becomes **read** when you:

- open it: tap its line in the log, use "Open link" from the long-press menu, or tap it on the
  Feeds screen;
- list it with `feed read`. Only the items that were printed count; anything beyond the 40 shown
  stays unread;
- open it with `feed open`;
- use "Mark all read" on the Feeds screen.

Seeing a line scroll by in the log does not mark it read, unless you turn on
`feeds.read-shown`. `feed all` lists items without changing them. The unread count is simply the
number of stored items still unread (up to 300 per feed are kept, unread ones first).

When you subscribe, everything the feed already contains is unread too. A busy feed can start at
20 or more unread items. Turn on `feeds.backlog-read` to count those as read, so only posts that
appear after you subscribed are unread.

### Feeds screen

`feeds` (or the **Feeds** tab in Settings) opens a screen for managing subscriptions: adding, removing and toggling feeds, intervals
and notifications, without typing ids. Tap a feed for its items (tap one to open it and mark it read).
The top bar refreshes everything and imports or exports **OPML**, so you can move subscriptions from
or to another feed reader. The `feed …` subcommands work too.

Feed behaviour is at the bottom of the same screen, under **Settings**: whether new items go to the
log and how many, the read / unread options above, refreshing on Wi-Fi only (`feeds.wifi`), and the
interval and notification setting new subscriptions start with (`feeds.interval`, `feeds.notify`).
Settings search finds them too.

## Per-feed settings

```sh
feed every 2 2h          # check feed #2 every 2 hours (minimum 15m; default 1h)
feed notify 2 on         # one notification per fetch with new items (off to stop)
feed off 2               # pause a feed; feed on 2 resumes it
feed rm 2                # unsubscribe and delete its items
```

## How fetching works

- One background job fetches the feeds that are due by their own interval. It runs only as often as your most frequent feed needs (every 15 minutes at most), and only when there
  is a network, or only on Wi-Fi with `feeds.wifi on`.
- Requests are conditional (ETag / Last-Modified), so an unchanged feed returns a `304` and isn't parsed.
- Up to 300 items are kept per feed (unread first, then newest).
- A failed scheduled fetch is only recorded in the feed's status (shown by `feed`). A manual fetch (`feed fetch`) also logs the error.
- Feeds run in the launcher process, not the script sandbox. For a feed you only want to see once,
  `rss <url>` prints up to 15 items into the log without subscribing.

## Feeds or a script?

Built-in feeds are the simplest option. For filtering, merging several feeds into one line, scoring
or a status line at the top, write a script with
[`rss.fresh(url)`](/scripting/api/#rssfreshurl), like the bundled
[`hn` script](/scripting/library/#hn).
