The guide calls each part of Kolahal by a name: *the header line*, *the suggestions*, *the key bar*. Here
they are, numbered on the app's own screens, so you know what each one is when you read about it.
The screenshots use the default theme, green.

## Home screen

From top to bottom: the **header** (1–4), the **log** (5–10) and the **prompt** (11–12). See
[Basics](/getting-started/basics/) for each.

<Annotated
	src={home}
	alt="Kolahal's home screen: the header with clock, date and items, a script's status line, the log with four groups, and the prompt"
	marks={[[16.7, 5.5], [68.6, 5.5], [13.3, 7.5], [66.7, 11.1], [50.0, 31.6], [62.2, 69.3], [3.1, 76.8], [86.7, 74.6], [38.9, 82.6], [42.8, 85.2], [12.2, 97.6], [88.9, 97.6], [94.4, 58.0]]}
>

1. **Clock and date.** Tap for your alarms.
2. **Header items**: unlocks, battery, open todos and the rest, each one tappable.
3. **⚙︎** opens Settings.
4. **Header line**: a script's line in the header (here, *countdown*'s). Tap it to run the script.
5. **Empty log space.** Tap it for the keyboard; double-tap it to lock the screen.
6. **A group**: a command and its output, with a gap above it.
7. **Coloured bar**: green beside commands, a script's colour beside its lines, red beside errors.
8. **Time**, on each group's first line.
9. **A command you typed.** Tap it to put it back in the prompt.
10. **Output.** Long-press any line for its actions.
11. **Prompt** (`$`). Tap the `$` for quick actions.
12. **Key bar button** (⌨): turns the key bar on or off.
13. **App selector handle**, on the edge, when the [app selector](#app-selector) is on.

</Annotated>

## While you type

The header shrinks so the suggestions have more room, and the key bar appears above the keyboard.

<Annotated
	src={typing}
	alt="Typing ma at the prompt: the one-line header, the suggestion list with Maps highlighted at the bottom, the prompt and the key bar"
	marks={[[61.1, 4.7], [88.9, 4.7], [66.7, 20.3], [35.6, 42.7], [80.0, 69.4], [28.9, 78.3], [17.8, 86.9], [79.4, 86.9], [57.2, 94.8]]}
>

1. **The header**, one line while you type, with how many status lines it hides.
2. **Expand**: shows the whole header.
3. **The log**, older output dimmed.
4. **Suggestions**: the apps, commands and settings that match.
5. **A command suggestion**, with its description.
6. **Best match**, highlighted at the bottom, nearest your thumb: Enter (⏎) opens it.
7. **The prompt**, with what you typed.
8. **Clear** (✕) empties the prompt.
9. **Key bar**: keys the keyboard leaves out (symbols, cursor, history), above the keyboard.

</Annotated>

## App selector

Turn it on in **Settings → App selector**. Touch the edge where the handle is, slide along it to a
letter, slide off onto the list to an app, and lift. [Basics](/getting-started/basics/#app-selector)
has the details.

**Sliding along the edge**

<Annotated
	src={selectorSlide}
	alt="Sliding along the right edge at G: the panel of apps on the left, Gmail and Google bright, the G bubble beside the thumb and the letters fanning out from the edge"
	marks={[[38.9, 33.9], [4.6, 53.6], [33.3, 62.4], [33.3, 82.2], [82.2, 60.2], [84.4, 55.8], [93.9, 73.7], [50.0, 15.8]]}
>

1. **Panel**: every app in one list, favourites (★) first or last, then A to Z.
2. **Section letter**, in the panel's margin.
3. **The letter's apps**, bright, starting level with your thumb.
4. **Other letters' apps**, faded back.
5. **Bubble**: the current letter, large, beside your thumb.
6. **Wave**: the letters next to it, bulging out so you see which way to go.
7. **Edge strip**: the part of the edge you slide on, with each letter at its position.
8. **Home**, dimmed while the selector is open.

</Annotated>

**Picking from the list**

<Annotated
	src={selectorPick}
	alt="Picking Gmail: its row magnified and filling with the theme colour, the rows beside it slightly larger"

1. **The app at your thumb**, magnified; move up and down to change it, lift to open it.
2. **Hold fill**: hold still and the row fills; when it's full, the app opens.
3. **Its neighbours**, a little larger, so you can see where you are.

</Annotated>

**Left open** (lift on the edge)

1. **The list, left open**, almost as wide as the screen, so your thumb reaches every row. Scroll it, tap an app, or long-press one for its actions.
2. **Space at the start of the list**, so the letter you tapped keeps its apps beside it.
3. **Edge strip**: slide along it again from here.

## Scripts tab

The script manager, with a card for each script. See [Scripting](/scripting/overview/).

<Annotated
	src={scripts}
	alt="The Scripts tab: filter chips, two tinted cards for scripts that are on, one with its details open, and a faded card for a script that is off"
	marks={[[17.8, 32.5], [76.7, 4.1], [93.1, 6.6], [61.1, 38.6], [5.6, 45.3], [42.2, 49.2], [62.8, 45.4], [75.7, 45.4], [87.8, 45.0], [82.2, 51.5], [81.1, 55.1], [43.3, 74.0], [25.6, 77.1], [63.6, 77.1], [36.7, 90.4], [80.0, 91.1]]}
>

1. **Filter chips**: commands, timers, events, failed, off, differs from the library and more, each
   with a count.
2. **Search** scripts by name.
3. **All details**: opens or closes every card's details.
4. **A script that's on**: its card is tinted in the theme's colour.
5. **Dot**: on, failed (red) or off (grey).
6. **Title and file name.**
7. **Run**, for a command script.
8. **Details arrow**: shows or hides this card's details.
9. **On/off switch.**
10. **Summary**: how it runs, its last run, and "edited" if you changed it.
11. **Its header line**, as it is now.
12. **Pills**: what it may use, filled when on and faded when off; tap one to switch it.
13. **Compare** your copy with the library version.
14. **Reset to library version.**
15. **A script that's off**: its card fades.
16. **Add script**: write one, or add one from the library, from other people or from a file.

</Annotated>

## Script editor

`script edit <name>`, or **Edit** in a script's sheet. See [Editor](/scripting/editor/).

<Annotated
	src={editor}
	alt="The script editor: the file name and the find, help, save, run and close buttons at the top, numbered lines of code, and the symbol bar"
	marks={[[11.1, 8.1], [27.7, 8.1], [39.6, 8.1], [54.7, 8.1], [71.8, 8.1], [90.0, 8.1], [3.4, 38.7], [11.7, 95.4], [70.0, 95.4]]}
>

1. **File name**, with `*` while there are unsaved changes.
2. **Find** (and replace).
3. **[?]**: the whole scripting API, in short.
4. **[save]**
5. **[run]**: saves, then runs it; its output appears under the code. A long press runs it with arguments, or as one of its events.
6. **[close]**: saves and closes.
7. **Line numbers.**
8. **Undo and redo.**
9. **Symbol bar**: the brackets and symbols code needs, above the keyboard.

</Annotated>
