The guide uses a name for each part of Kolahal, such as *the pinned header*, *the suggestions* and *the key bar*.
This page shows each part, numbered on screenshots of the app. Then you know what each name means when you read about it.
The screenshots use the default theme, rose-pine.

## Home screen

From top to bottom, the home screen has the **header** (1–2), the **pinned header** (3), the **log** (4–9) and the
**prompt** (10–11). [Basics](/getting-started/basics/) explains each one.

<Annotated
	src={home}
	alt="Kolahal's home screen: the header with clock, date, alarm, open todos and battery, a pinned script's line under it, the log with four groups, and the prompt"
	marks={[[41.0, 2.7], [56.5, 2.7], [45.0, 6.4], [50.0, 30.0], [62.0, 71.0], [3.1, 79.0], [93.0, 76.9], [37.0, 86.3], [45.0, 88.7], [9.5, 95.1], [88.9, 95.1], [94.5, 60.0]]}
>

1. **Clock and date.** Tap to see your alarms.
2. **Header items**: the parts on the header's right side (here, the next alarm, open todos and the battery). Tap one to open it.
3. **Pinned header**: the line of a script that you pinned (here, the *countdown* script). Tap it to run the script. Long-press it for more.
4. **Empty log space.** Tap it to open the keyboard. Double-tap it to lock the screen (not in the Play build).
5. **A group**: a command and its output, with a gap above it.
6. **Colored bar**: the accent color next to commands, the script's color next to a script's lines, and red next to errors.
7. **Time**, on the first line of each group.
8. **A command you typed.** Tap it to put it back in the prompt.
9. **Output.** Long-press any line to see its actions.
10. **Prompt** (`$`). Tap the `$` to open quick actions.
11. **Key bar button** (⌨): turns the key bar on or off.
12. **App selector handle**, on the edge of the screen, when the [app selector](#app-selector) is on.

</Annotated>

## While you type

While you type, the pinned header hides to give the suggestions more room. The key bar appears above the
keyboard.

<Annotated
	src={typing}
	alt="Typing ma at the prompt: the header, the log, the suggestion list with Maps highlighted at the bottom, the prompt and the key bar"
	marks={[[48.0, 4.2], [50.0, 32.8], [30.0, 70.9], [28.0, 81.5], [17.8, 88.9], [79.4, 88.9], [57.2, 95.4]]}
>

1. **The header**. It doesn't change while you type.
2. **The log.**
3. **Suggestions**: the apps, commands and settings that match what you typed. A command shows its description.
4. **Best match**, highlighted at the bottom, nearest your thumb. Press Enter (⏎) to open it.
5. **The prompt**, with what you typed.
6. **Clear** (✕) empties the prompt.
7. **Key bar**: keys that the keyboard doesn't have (symbols, cursor, history), above the keyboard.

</Annotated>

## App selector

The [first-run tour](/getting-started/setup/#the-tour) turns it on, and its page picks the edge or turns it off. **Settings → App selector** does the same later. To use it:

1. Touch the edge of the screen where the letters are (or the handle).
2. Slide along the edge to a letter.
3. Slide off the edge onto the list, to an app.
4. Lift your finger.

[Basics](/getting-started/basics/#app-selector) has the details.

**Sliding along the edge**

<Annotated
	src={selectorSlide}
	alt="Sliding along the right edge at G: the list of apps on the left with Gmail and Google magnified, the G bubble beside the thumb, the letters fanning out from the edge, and the header and the prompt still showing"
	marks={[[40.0, 33.6], [3.5, 51.3], [37.0, 56.6], [73.1, 56.3], [84.0, 50.5], [93.5, 76.3], [50.0, 2.7], [11.1, 95.1]]}
>

1. **The list**: every app in one list, in the log's place. The log fades out behind it. Your favorites (★) have a section of their own, last when the selector is at the bottom of the edge and first otherwise.
2. **Section letter**, in the list's margin.
3. **The apps at your thumb**, magnified. With **Slide through** set to `letters`, the letter's apps start at the same height as your thumb and the other letters' apps fade instead.
4. **Bubble**: the current letter, large, next to your thumb.
5. **Wave**: the letters near the current one stick out, so you can see which way to go.
6. **Edge strip**: the part of the edge you slide on. Each letter has its own position on it.
7. **Header**, still showing while the selector is open.
8. **Prompt**, still showing while the selector is open.

</Annotated>

**Picking from the list**

<Annotated
	src={selectorPick}

1. **The app at your thumb**, highlighted and magnified. Move up or down to pick another app. Lift your finger to open it. If you hold still, the row fills with color, and when it's full, the app opens.
2. **The rows next to it**, a little larger, so you can see where you are.
3. **The edge**, empty while you pick. Slide back onto it to cancel.

1. **The list, left open**. It reaches almost to the edge, so your thumb can reach every row. Scroll it, tap an app, or long-press an app to see its actions.
2. **Letters on the edge**: you can slide along them again from here.

## Action sheet and quick actions

**Long-press** a line in the log to open its action sheet. **Tap the `$`** to open quick actions.

you can also tap it in the header. See [Themes & settings](/guides/themes-and-settings/).

## Scripts tab

The script manager, with a card for each script. See [Scripting](/scripting/overview/).

<Annotated
	src={scripts}
	alt="The Scripts tab: a search box with the Filters and sort buttons, tinted cards for scripts that are on, one with its details open, and a faded card for a script that is off"
	marks={[[60.0, 32.8], [78.7, 32.8], [90.1, 32.8], [92.9, 8.8], [50.0, 41.9], [9.6, 62.4], [38.0, 62.4], [61.7, 63.1], [74.4, 63.1], [86.7, 63.1], [72.0, 66.5], [58.0, 69.7], [93.0, 75.0], [38.0, 89.2], [30.0, 95.8], [79.0, 92.5]]}
>

1. **Search** scripts by name.
2. **Filters**: a sheet of chips by status, how a script runs (command, timer, event, pinned), source,
   library version, what it uses and language. Each chip shows a count.
3. **Sort**: by name, by last run, or recently changed first.
4. **All details**: opens or closes the details of every card.
5. **A script that's on**: its card is tinted with the theme's color.
6. **Dot**: on, failed (red) or off (gray).
7. **Title and file name.**
8. **Run**, for a command script.
9. **Details arrow**: shows or hides the details of this card.
10. **On/off switch.**
11. **Summary**: how the script runs and when it last ran.
12. **Its status line**, as it is now.
13. **Details**: what it does, how to call it, and when it runs next. For a library script you
    changed, **Compare** and **Reset to library version** are here too.
14. **Pills**: what the script may use. A pill is filled when on and faded when off. Tap a pill to turn it on or off.
    *Pinned* puts the script's status line in the pinned header.
15. **A script that's off**: its card is faded.
16. **Add script**: write a new script, or add one from the library, from other people or from a file.

</Annotated>

## Script editor

To open the editor, run `script edit <name>`, or tap **Edit** in a script's sheet. See [Editor](/scripting/editor/).

<Annotated
	src={editor}
	alt="The script editor: the file name and the find, help, save, run and close buttons at the top, numbered lines of code, and the symbol bar"
	marks={[[11.1, 7.8], [47.4, 7.8], [59.1, 7.8], [70.7, 7.8], [82.6, 7.8], [94.3, 7.8], [3.4, 38.7], [11.7, 95.3], [70.0, 95.3]]}
>

1. **File name**. It shows `*` while there are unsaved changes.
2. **Find** (and replace).
3. **API reference** (the question mark): opens the [reference](/scripting/editor/#reference), a page with the whole scripting API, the `@` lines, the widget and log examples, and the icons.
4. **Save**
5. **Run** (the play button): saves the script, then runs it. Its output appears under the code. Long-press to run it with arguments, or as one of its events.
6. **Close**: saves and closes the editor.
7. **Line numbers.**
8. **Undo and redo.**
9. **Symbol bar**: the brackets and symbols code needs, above the keyboard.

</Annotated>
