The guide calls each part of Kolahal by a name: *the pinned header*, *the suggestions*, *the key bar*. Here
they are, numbered on the app's own screens, so you know what each one is when you read about it.
The screenshots use the default theme, green.

## Home screen

From top to bottom: the **header** (1–2), the **pinned header** (3), the **log** (4–9) and the
**prompt** (10–11). See [Basics](/getting-started/basics/) for each.

<Annotated
	src={home}
	alt="Kolahal's home screen: the header line with clock, date, alarm, open todos and battery, a pinned script's line under it, the log with four groups, and the prompt"
	marks={[[17.0, 5.3], [79.0, 5.3], [69.0, 8.1], [50.0, 30.0], [62.0, 71.0], [3.1, 79.0], [86.7, 75.6], [38.9, 83.6], [42.8, 86.1], [9.5, 97.7], [88.9, 97.7], [94.5, 60.0]]}
>

1. **Clock and date.** Tap for your alarms.
2. **Header items**: the two you chose (here, the next alarm and open todos) and the battery. Tap one to open it.
3. **Pinned header**: the line of a script you pinned (here, *countdown*'s). Tap it to run the script; long-press it for more.
4. **Empty log space.** Tap it for the keyboard; double-tap it to lock the screen.
5. **A group**: a command and its output, with a gap above it.
6. **Coloured bar**: green beside commands, a script's colour beside its lines, red beside errors.
7. **Time**, on each group's first line.
8. **A command you typed.** Tap it to put it back in the prompt.
9. **Output.** Long-press any line for its actions.
10. **Prompt** (`$`). Tap the `$` for quick actions.
11. **Key bar button** (⌨): turns the key bar on or off.
12. **App selector handle**, on the edge, when the [app selector](#app-selector) is on.

</Annotated>

## While you type

The pinned header steps aside so the suggestions have more room, and the key bar appears above the
keyboard.

<Annotated
	src={typing}
	alt="Typing ma at the prompt: the header line, the log, the suggestion list with Maps highlighted at the bottom, the prompt and the key bar"
	marks={[[48.0, 4.1], [50.0, 32.8], [30.0, 59.6], [80.0, 75.0], [28.0, 82.6], [17.8, 89.9], [79.4, 89.9], [57.2, 96.9]]}
>

1. **The header**, the same while you type.
2. **The log.**
3. **Suggestions**: the apps, commands and settings that match.
4. **A command suggestion**, with its description.
5. **Best match**, highlighted at the bottom, nearest your thumb: Enter (⏎) opens it.
6. **The prompt**, with what you typed.
7. **Clear** (✕) empties the prompt.
8. **Key bar**: keys the keyboard leaves out (symbols, cursor, history), above the keyboard.

</Annotated>

## App selector

Turn it on in **Settings → App selector**. Touch the edge where the handle is, slide along it to a
letter, slide off onto the list to an app, and lift. [Basics](/getting-started/basics/#app-selector)
has the details.

**Sliding along the edge**

<Annotated
	src={selectorSlide}
	alt="Sliding along the right edge at G: the panel of apps on the left, Gmail and Google bright, the G bubble beside the thumb and the letters fanning out from the edge"
	marks={[[40.0, 33.6], [3.5, 55.8], [30.0, 64.5], [30.0, 79.0], [83.0, 60.0], [85.0, 56.0], [94.0, 74.0], [60.0, 20.0]]}
>

1. **Panel**: every app in one list, favourites (★) first or last, then A to Z.
2. **Section letter**, in the panel's margin.
3. **The letter's apps**, bright, starting level with your thumb.
4. **Other letters' apps**, faded back.
5. **Bubble**: the current letter, large, beside your thumb.
6. **Wave**: the letters next to it, bulging out so you see which way to go.
7. **Edge strip**: the part of the edge you slide on, with each letter at its position.
8. **Home**, dimmed while the selector is open.

</Annotated>

**Picking from the list**

<Annotated

1. **The app at your thumb**, magnified; move up and down to change it, lift to open it.
2. **Hold fill**: hold still and the row fills; when it's full, the app opens.
3. **Its neighbours**, a little larger, so you can see where you are.

**Left open** (lift on the edge)

1. **The list, left open**, almost as wide as the screen, so your thumb reaches every row. Scroll it, tap an app, or long-press one for its actions.

## Scripts tab

The script manager, with a card for each script. See [Scripting](/scripting/overview/).

<Annotated
	src={scripts}
	alt="The Scripts tab: filter chips, two tinted cards for scripts that are on, one with its details open, and a faded card for a script that is off"
	marks={[[19.0, 35.6], [74.5, 8.8], [93.0, 11.5], [59.0, 41.0], [8.7, 48.1], [38.0, 51.5], [62.7, 48.1], [75.5, 48.1], [88.0, 47.9], [82.2, 53.9], [79.2, 57.3], [34.3, 75.2], [25.5, 78.2], [63.4, 78.2], [47.0, 88.7], [80.0, 91.6]]}
>

1. **Filter chips**: commands, timers, events, pinned, failed, off, differs from the library and
   more, each with a count.
2. **Search** scripts by name.
3. **All details**: opens or closes every card's details.
4. **A script that's on**: its card is tinted in the theme's colour.
5. **Dot**: on, failed (red) or off (grey).
6. **Title and file name.**
7. **Run**, for a command script.
8. **Details arrow**: shows or hides this card's details.
9. **On/off switch.**
10. **Summary**: how it runs, its last run, and "edited" if you changed it.
11. **Its status line**, as it is now.
12. **Pills**: what it may use, filled when on and faded when off; tap one to switch it. *pinned*
    puts its status line in the pinned header.
13. **Compare** your copy with the library version.
14. **Reset to library version.**
15. **A script that's off**: its card fades.
16. **Add script**: write one, or add one from the library, from other people or from a file.

</Annotated>

## Script editor

`script edit <name>`, or **Edit** in a script's sheet. See [Editor](/scripting/editor/).

<Annotated
	src={editor}
	alt="The script editor: the file name and the find, help, save, run and close buttons at the top, numbered lines of code, and the symbol bar"
	marks={[[11.1, 8.1], [27.7, 8.1], [39.6, 8.1], [54.7, 8.1], [71.8, 8.1], [90.0, 8.1], [3.4, 38.7], [11.7, 95.4], [70.0, 95.4]]}
>

1. **File name**, with `*` while there are unsaved changes.
2. **Find** (and replace).
3. **[?]**: the whole scripting API, in short.
4. **[save]**
5. **[run]**: saves, then runs it; its output appears under the code. A long press runs it with arguments, or as one of its events.
6. **[close]**: saves and closes.
7. **Line numbers.**
8. **Undo and redo.**
9. **Symbol bar**: the brackets and symbols code needs, above the keyboard.

</Annotated>
