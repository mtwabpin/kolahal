---
title: Test on Google Play
description: Help Kolahal reach Google Play by joining its closed test, installing it from the Play Store and keeping it for 14 days.
---

Kolahal is coming to Google Play. Before Google releases it to everyone, at least 12 people must
test it there for 14 days in a row. If you have an Android phone (Android 9 or newer) and a Google
account, you can be one of them.

## Join

1. **Join the group** [groups.google.com/g/kolahal](https://groups.google.com/g/kolahal). Use the
   Google account that your phone's Play Store uses. Google Play uses the group to know who the testers
   are.
2. **Become a tester**: open
   [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.mtwabpin.kolahal](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.mtwabpin.kolahal)
   with the same account and tap **Become a tester**.
3. **Install Kolahal** from its
   [Play Store page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mtwabpin.kolahal). After step 2, it can
   take a few minutes before Kolahal appears there.
4. **Keep it for 14 days**: stay in the test and keep Kolahal installed. It helps most if you make it your home app and
   use it, but you don't have to.

## What you get

You get the Play build. It has everything in Kolahal except the screen lock, calls in the log and `update`.
It finds people with Android's contact picker ([the Play build](/getting-started/install/#the-play-build)).
It updates through the Play Store, like any other app. [First run & setup](/getting-started/setup/) explains the rest.

Kolahal from GitHub and Kolahal from Google Play are the same app, signed with the same key. So one
installs over the other, and you keep your data. An update from Google Play installs the Play build.
To get the screen lock, calls in the log and `update` back, install the full APK from GitHub again.

## Tell us what you find

- If something is broken or strange, [report a bug](https://github.com/mtwabpin/kolahal/issues/new/choose)
  or tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/3D25w6gXR5).
- If you have an idea, use the [feature request form](https://github.com/mtwabpin/kolahal/issues/new/choose) or
  Discord.
- Testers can also send feedback on Kolahal's Play Store page. Only the developer sees it.

To leave the test, open the tester link from step 2 again and tap **Leave the program**. Kolahal
stays installed until you uninstall it.
