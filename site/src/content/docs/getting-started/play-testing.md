---
title: Test on Google Play
description: Help Kolahal reach Google Play by joining its closed test, installing it from the Play Store and keeping it for 14 days.
---

Kolahal is coming to Google Play. Before Google releases it to everyone, at least 12 people have to
test it there for 14 days in a row. If you have an Android phone (Android 9 or newer) and a Google
account, you can be one of them.

## Join

1. **Join the group** [groups.google.com/g/kolahal](https://groups.google.com/g/kolahal) with the
   Google account your phone's Play Store uses. The group is how Google Play knows who the testers
   are.
2. **Become a tester**: open
   [play.google.com/apps/testing/com.mtwabpin.kolahal](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.mtwabpin.kolahal)
   with that account and tap **Become a tester**.
3. **Install Kolahal** from its
   [Play Store page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mtwabpin.kolahal). It can
   take a few minutes after step 2 to show up there.
4. **Keep it for 14 days**: stay in the test with Kolahal installed. Making it your home app and
   using it helps most, but isn't required.

## What you get

The Play build: everything in Kolahal except the screen lock, calls in the log and `update`. It finds people with Android's contact picker
([the Play build](/getting-started/install/#the-play-build)). Updates come through the Play Store
like any app's. [First run & setup](/getting-started/setup/) walks you through the rest.

Kolahal from GitHub and from Google Play are the same app, signed with the same key, so one
installs over the other and your data is kept. An update from Google Play installs the Play build;
install the full APK from GitHub again to get the screen lock, calls in the log and `update` back.

## Tell us what you find

- Something broken or odd: [report a bug](https://github.com/mtwabpin/kolahal/issues/new/choose),
  or say so on [Discord](https://discord.gg/3D25w6gXR5).
- An idea: the [feature request form](https://github.com/mtwabpin/kolahal/issues/new/choose), or
  Discord.
- On Kolahal's Play Store page, testers can also send feedback that only the developer sees.

To leave the test, open the tester link from step 2 again and tap **Leave the program**. Kolahal
stays installed until you uninstall it.
